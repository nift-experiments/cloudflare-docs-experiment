


To enroll your device using the client GUI:

<div class="nb-tabs" data-nb-tabs><div role="tablist" aria-label="Options" data-nb-tabs-list></div><div data-nb-tabs-panels>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Version 2026.2+">
<ol>
<li><a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/download/">Download</a> and install the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Launch the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>On the <strong>What would you like to use the Cloudflare One Client for?</strong> screen, select <strong>Zero Trust security</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter your <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="team name">team name</span>.</li>
<li>Complete the authentication steps required by your organization.</li>
</ol>
<p>Once authenticated, you will see a Success page and a dialog prompting you to open the Cloudflare One Client.</p>
<ol start="6">
<li>Select <strong>Open the Cloudflare One Client</strong> to complete the registration.</li>
</ol>
</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Version 2026.1 and earlier">
<ol start="7">
<li><a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/download/">Download</a> and install the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Launch the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Select the Cloudflare logo in the menu bar.</li>
<li>Select the gear icon.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Preferences</strong> &gt; <strong>Account</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Login with Cloudflare Zero Trust</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter your <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="team name">team name</span>.</li>
<li>Complete the authentication steps required by your organization.</li>
</ol>
<p>Once authenticated, you will see a Success page and a dialog prompting you to open the Cloudflare One Client.</p>
<ol start="15">
<li>Select <strong>Open Cloudflare WARP.app</strong> to complete the registration.</li>
</ol>
</section>

</div></div>
