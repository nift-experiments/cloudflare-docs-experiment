<li>Create a list of device serial numbers that you do not want to inspect.</li>
</ol>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/gateway/lists \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;description&quot;: &quot;The serial numbers for administrators&quot;,
&quot;items&quot;: [
  {
    &quot;value&quot;: &quot;8GE8721RE&quot;
  }
],
&quot;name&quot;: &quot;Admin Serial Numbers&quot;,
&quot;type&quot;: &quot;SERIAL&quot;
}&#x27;</code></pre>
<ol start="2">
<li>Create a Do Not Inspect policy that checks the device against the list of serial numbers.</li>
</ol>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/gateway/rules \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;name&quot;: &quot;Do not inspect corporate devices&quot;,
&quot;traffic&quot;: &quot;&quot;,
&quot;identity&quot;: &quot;&quot;,
&quot;device_posture&quot;: &quot;any(device_posture.checks.passed[*] in {\&quot;&lt;SERIAL_NUMBER_LIST_UUID&gt;\&quot;})&quot;,
&quot;action&quot;: &quot;off&quot;,
&quot;precedence&quot;: 14002,
&quot;enabled&quot;: true,
&quot;filters&quot;: [
  &quot;http&quot;
]
}&#x27;</code></pre>
