<p>To delete a monitor in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>Load Balancing</strong>.</li>
<li>Select the <strong>Monitors</strong> tab.</li>
<li>On a specific monitor, select <strong>Delete</strong>.</li>
</ol>
