

To fetch the latest health status of all pools, use the [List Pools](/api/resources/load_balancers/subresources/pools/methods/list/) command, paying attention to the `healthy` value for pools and origins (endpoints).

For troubleshooting a specific pool's health, use the [Pool Health Details](/api/resources/load_balancers/subresources/pools/subresources/health/methods/get/) command.
