<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To confirm pool health using the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>Load Balancing</strong>.</li>
<li>Select the <strong>Pools</strong> tab.</li>
<li>For pools and individual endpoints, review the values in the <strong>Health</strong> and <strong>Endpoint Health</strong> columns.</li>
</ol>
<p>For more information on pool and endpoint health statuses, refer to <a href="/load-balancing/understand-basics/health-details/#how-a-pool-becomes-unhealthy">How a pool becomes unhealthy</a>.</p>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To fetch the latest health status of all pools, use the <a href="/api/resources/load_balancers/subresources/pools/methods/list/">List Pools</a> command, paying attention to the <code>healthy</code> value for pools and origins (endpoints).</p>
<p>For troubleshooting a specific pool's health, use the <a href="/api/resources/load_balancers/subresources/pools/subresources/health/methods/get/">Pool Health Details</a> command.</p>
