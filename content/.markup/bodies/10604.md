Make sure each IPsec tunnel has a unique combination of a [Cloudflare endpoint and customer endpoint](/magic-transit/how-to/configure-tunnel-endpoints/). If this combination is not unique among your IPsec tunnels, you should use one of the custom IKE formats (`ID_RFC822_ADDR`, `ID_FQDN`, or `ID_KEY_ID`) to specify the tunnel ID and account ID. This helps Cloudflare link the IKE packet to the right IPsec tunnel for tasks like authentication.
