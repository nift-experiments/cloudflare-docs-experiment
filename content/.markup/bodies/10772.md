<ol start="2">
<li>Select your Mesh node.</li>
<li>Go to the <strong>Routes</strong> tab.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add route</strong>, then select <strong>Private hostname</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter the fully qualified domain name (FQDN) you want to route through this node (for example, <code>wiki.internal.local</code>).</li>
</ol>
<details class="nb-details"><summary>Hostname format restrictions</summary><div class="nb-details-body">
<ul>
<li><strong>Character limit:</strong> Must be less than 255 characters.</li>
<li><strong>Supported wildcards:</strong> A single wildcard (<code>*</code>) is allowed, and it must represent a full DNS label.
Example: <code>*.internal.local</code></li>
<li><strong>Unsupported wildcards:</strong> The following wildcard formats are not supported:</li>
<li>Partial wildcards such as <code>*-dev.internal.local</code> or <code>dev-*.internal.local</code>.</li>
<li>Wildcards in the middle, such as <code>foo*bar.internal.local</code> or <code>foo.*.internal.local</code>.</li>
<li>Multiple wildcards in the hostname, such as <code>*.*.internal.local</code>.</li>
<li><strong>Wildcard trimming</strong>: Leading wildcards (<code>*</code>) are trimmed off and an implicit dot (<code>.</code>) is assumed. For example, <code>*.internal.local</code> is saved as <code>internal.local</code> but will match all subdomains at the wildcard level (covers <code>foo.internal.local</code> but not <code>foo.bar.internal.local</code>).</li>
<li><strong>Dot trimming:</strong> Leading and ending dots (<code>.</code>) are allowed but trimmed off.</li>
</ul>
</div></details>
