<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to <strong>Networking</strong> &gt; <strong>Mesh</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select an HA-enabled node.</li>
<li>Select the tab for the passive replica you want to promote.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Promote to active</strong>.</li>
<li>In the confirmation dialog, select <strong>Promote to active</strong> to confirm.</li>
</ol>
<p>Traffic reroutes to the promoted replica immediately. The previous active replica switches to passive standby.</p>

<p>To manually trigger failover, send a <code>PUT</code> request with the <code>client_id</code> of the replica you want to promote:</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">curl -X PUT &quot;https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/warp_connector/{node_id}/failover&quot; \
  -H &quot;Authorization: Bearer {api_token}&quot; \
  -H &quot;Content-Type: application/json&quot; \
  -d '{
    &quot;client_id&quot;: &quot;e07272a6-21fc-11f1-8997-e28f01ba3991&quot;
  }'
</code></pre>
<p>Get the <code>client_id</code> from the <a href="#view-replicas">connections endpoint</a>. Use the <code>id</code> field of the replica you want to promote.</p>
