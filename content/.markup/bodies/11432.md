
<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li>Log in to your Cloudflare account:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">npx wrangler login
</code></pre>
<ol start="2">
<li>Create a bucket named <code>my-bucket</code>:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">npx wrangler r2 bucket create my-bucket
</code></pre>
<p>If prompted, select the account you want to create the bucket in.</p>
<ol start="3">
<li>Verify the bucket was created:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">npx wrangler r2 bucket list
</code></pre>
