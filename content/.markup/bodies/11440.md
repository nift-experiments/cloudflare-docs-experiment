

[rclone](/r2/examples/rclone/) is ideal for bulk uploads, migrations, and syncing directories.

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li><a href="https://rclone.org/install/">Install rclone</a> (version 1.59 or later).</li>
<li>Configure a new remote:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">rclone config
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Create new remote by selecting <code>n</code>.</li>
<li>Name your remote <code>r2</code></li>
<li>Select <strong>Amazon S3 Compliant Storage Providers</strong> as the storage type.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Cloudflare R2</strong> as the provider.</li>
<li>Select whether you would like to enter AWS credentials manually, or get it from the runtime environment.</li>
<li>Enter your Access Key ID and Secret Access Key when prompted.</li>
<li>Select the region to connect to (optional).</li>
<li>Provide your S3 API endpoint.</li>
</ol>

</div>
