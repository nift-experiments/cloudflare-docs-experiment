

The [AWS CLI](/r2/examples/aws/aws-cli/) works with R2 by specifying a custom endpoint.

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li><a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cli/latest/userguide/getting-started-install.html">Install the AWS CLI</a> for your operating system.</li>
<li>Configure your credentials:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">aws configure
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>When prompted, enter:</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>AWS Access Key ID</strong>: Your R2 Access Key ID</li>
<li><strong>AWS Secret Access Key</strong>: Your R2 Secret Access Key</li>
<li><strong>Default region name</strong>: <code>auto</code></li>
<li><strong>Default output format</strong>: <code>json</code> (or press Enter to skip)</li>
</ul>

</div>
