<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="rclone">

<p><a href="/r2/examples/rclone/">rclone</a> is ideal for bulk uploads, migrations, and syncing directories.</p>
<div class="nb-steps">
<ol>
<li><a href="https://rclone.org/install/">Install rclone</a> (version 1.59 or later).</li>
<li>Configure a new remote:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">rclone config
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Create new remote by selecting <code>n</code>.</li>
<li>Name your remote <code>r2</code></li>
<li>Select <strong>Amazon S3 Compliant Storage Providers</strong> as the storage type.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Cloudflare R2</strong> as the provider.</li>
<li>Select whether you would like to enter AWS credentials manually, or get it from the runtime environment.</li>
<li>Enter your Access Key ID and Secret Access Key when prompted.</li>
<li>Select the region to connect to (optional).</li>
<li>Provide your S3 API endpoint.</li>
</ol>
</div>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="AWS CLI">

<p>The <a href="/r2/examples/aws/aws-cli/">AWS CLI</a> works with R2 by specifying a custom endpoint.</p>
<div class="nb-steps">
<ol>
<li><a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cli/latest/userguide/getting-started-install.html">Install the AWS CLI</a> for your operating system.</li>
<li>Configure your credentials:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">aws configure
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>When prompted, enter:</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>AWS Access Key ID</strong>: Your R2 Access Key ID</li>
<li><strong>AWS Secret Access Key</strong>: Your R2 Secret Access Key</li>
<li><strong>Default region name</strong>: <code>auto</code></li>
<li><strong>Default output format</strong>: <code>json</code> (or press Enter to skip)</li>
</ul>
</div>
