
Initialize the RealtimeKit SDK by creating an instance of `RealtimeKitClient`.

<pre><code class="language-swift">let meeting = RealtimeKitiOSClientBuilder().build()
</code></pre>


   Add the required listeners to receive callbacks for meeting events.

<pre><code class="language-swift">meeting.addMeetingRoomEventListener(meetingRoomEventListener: self)
meeting.addParticipantsEventListener(participantsEventListener: self)
meeting.addSelfEventListener(selfEventListener: self)
</code></pre>


   Configure the meeting properties in the `RtkMeetingInfo` class with a valid participant `authToken` from the [Add participant API](/api/resources/realtime_kit/subresources/meetings/methods/add_participant/).

<pre><code class="language-swift">let meetingInfo = RtkMeetingInfo(authToken: authToken,
                                  enableAudio: true,
                                  enableVideo: true)
</code></pre>


   Initialize the meeting by calling the `doInit()` method. This establishes a connection with the RealtimeKit meeting server.

<pre><code class="language-swift">meeting.doInit(meetingInfo: meetingInfo, onSuccess: {}, onFailure: {_ in})
</code></pre>
