Summarization requires a transcript. To generate summaries automatically when a meeting ends, turn on [real-time transcription](/realtime/realtimekit/ai/transcription/#real-time-transcription) or [post-meeting transcription](/realtime/realtimekit/ai/transcription/#post-meeting-transcription), and set `summarize_on_end: true`. With post-meeting transcription, RealtimeKit generates the summary after the transcript is available.
