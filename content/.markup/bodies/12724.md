If stable and defined source IPv4 addresses cannot be assigned to the on-premises DNS servers, service providers can instead use unique destination location endpoints. Each location is assigned a distinct [DoT](/cloudflare-one/networks/resolvers-and-proxies/dns/locations/dns-resolver-ips/#dns-over-tls-dot) and [DoH](/cloudflare-one/networks/resolvers-and-proxies/dns/locations/dns-resolver-ips/#dns-over-https-doh) hostname, as well as a unique [destination IPv6 address](/cloudflare-one/networks/resolvers-and-proxies/dns/locations/dns-resolver-ips/#ipv4ipv6-address). Additionally, Cloudflare can provide unique [destination IPv4 addresses upon request](/cloudflare-one/networks/resolvers-and-proxies/dns/locations/dns-resolver-ips/#dns-resolver-ip).
