

**Context:**

You configured a Page Rule adding a `CF-IPCountry` HTTP header, for all requests addressed at any subdomain of `example.com` and the `example.com` domain itself:

- **URL**: `*example.com/*`
- **Setting**: _IP Geolocation Header_
- **Value**: On

**How to migrate**:

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li><a href="/rules/transform/managed-transforms/configure/">Turn on the <strong>Add visitor location headers</strong> Managed Transform</a> — a Transform Rules feature — to add the <code>CF-IPCountry</code> and other location headers to all requests.</li>
<li>Turn off your existing Page Rule and validate the behavior of the Managed Transform.</li>
<li>If your tests succeed, delete the existing Page Rule.</li>
</ol>
