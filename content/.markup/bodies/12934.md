

**Context:**

You configured a Page Rule adding a `True-Client-IP` HTTP header for all requests addressed at any subdomain of `example.com` and the `example.com` domain itself:

- **URL**: `*example.com/*`
- **Setting**: _True Client IP Header_
- **Value**: On

**How to migrate**:

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li><a href="/rules/transform/managed-transforms/configure/">Turn on the <strong>Add &quot;True-Client-IP&quot; header</strong> Managed Transform</a> — a Transform Rules feature — to add the <code>True-Client-IP</code> header to all requests.</li>
<li>Turn off your existing Page Rule and validate the behavior of the Managed Transform.</li>
