

To delete a page rule in the dashboard:

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Page Rules</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For a specific rule, select <strong>X</strong>. Then, select <strong>Delete</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</div>
