This page documents options on today's stable `@cloudflare/sandbox` package.

In the **1.0 preview** (`@next`), `enableDefaultSession` and transport selection are removed. `sleepAfter`, `keepAlive`, `containerTimeouts`, and `normalizeId` still apply. Refer to [Sandbox lifecycle](/sandbox/1-0-preview/lifecycle/) and [Migrate](/sandbox/1-0-preview/migrate/).
