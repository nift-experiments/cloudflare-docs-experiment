Use **quick tunnels** for local development, demos, and short-lived `.workers.dev` deployments where you do not need a stable URL. Use **named tunnels** for everything else — they are the recommended option for production traffic, webhook receivers, OAuth callbacks, and any URL that needs to be bookmarked. [`exposePort()`](/sandbox/api/ports/) remains an alternative when you want the Worker itself (rather than Cloudflare's edge) to front the request.
