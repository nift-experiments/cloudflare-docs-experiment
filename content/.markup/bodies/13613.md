For most public-URL use cases — development, `.workers.dev` deployments, and production traffic — [`sandbox.tunnels`](/sandbox/api/tunnels/) is the recommended option. Use named tunnels for stable hostnames on a zone you control, or quick tunnels for zero-config `*.trycloudflare.com` URLs. `exposePort()` is appropriate when you want the Worker itself to front the request (for example, to inject authentication, rewrite responses, or call sandbox-only RPC methods on the same hostname).
