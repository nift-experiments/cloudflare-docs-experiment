This page documents lifecycle helpers on today's stable `@cloudflare/sandbox` package.

In the **1.0 preview** (`@next`), remove transport options on `getSandbox()` and do not rely on `enableDefaultSession`. Keep `sleepAfter`, `keepAlive`, `containerTimeouts`, `normalizeId`, and `destroy` when you need them. Process and terminal handles are container-local after stop or replace. Refer to [Sandbox lifecycle](/sandbox/1-0-preview/lifecycle/) and [Migrate](/sandbox/1-0-preview/migrate/).
