<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Secrets Store</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Search for the secret you would like to duplicate within the existing secrets list.</li>
<li>Select the three dots next to the secret and choose <strong>Duplicate</strong>.</li>
<li>Edit the <strong>Secret name</strong>, <strong>Permission scope</strong>, or <strong>Comment</strong>, according to your needs.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong> to confirm.</li>
</ol>
