<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Secrets Store</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Search for the secret you would like to delete within the existing secrets list.</li>
<li>Select the three dots next to the secret and choose <strong>Delete</strong>.</li>
<li>Type in the secret name and select <strong>Delete</strong> to confirm.</li>
</ol>
