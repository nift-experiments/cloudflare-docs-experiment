The process described on this page leverages automated <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="letter of agency">LOA</span> generation. If you set `delegate_loa_creation` to `false`, you have to manually upload your LOA, make a [PATCH request](/api/resources/addressing/subresources/prefixes/methods/edit/) once the prefix is approved, and contact your account team - which is more prone to error and increases the onboarding time.
