<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To enable <strong>Enhanced HTTP/2 Prioritization</strong> in the Cloudflare dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log into the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com">Cloudflare dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Select your account and zone.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Speed</strong> &gt; <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Protocol Optimization</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Enhanced HTTP/2 Prioritization</strong>, switch the toggle to <strong>On</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To enable <strong>Enhanced HTTP/2 Prioritization</strong> using the Cloudflare API, send a <a href="/api/resources/zones/subresources/settings/methods/edit/"><code>PATCH</code> request</a> with <code>h2_prioritization</code> as the setting name in the URI path, and the <code>value</code> parameter set to <code>&quot;on&quot;</code>.</p>

</section>
