

To enable or disable **Rocket Loader** in the dashboard:

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the **Speed** > **Settings** page.

<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>

2. Go to **Content Optimization**.
3. For **Rocket Loader**, switch the toggle to **On**.


If you have a <div class="nb-interactive-component" data-cf-component="GlossaryTooltip">

<p>Content Security Policy (CSP)</p>

</div> in place for your domain, you will need to [update your headers](/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/content-security-policies/#product-requirements) to support Rocket Loader.
