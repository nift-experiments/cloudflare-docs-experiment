<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To enable or disable <strong>Rocket Loader</strong> in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Speed</strong> &gt; <strong>Settings</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Go to <strong>Content Optimization</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Rocket Loader</strong>, switch the toggle to <strong>On</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>If you have a <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="content security policy (CSP)">Content Security Policy (CSP)</span> in place for your domain, you will need to <a href="/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/content-security-policies/#product-requirements">update your headers</a> to support Rocket Loader.</p>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To enable or disable <strong>Rocket Loader</strong> with the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/zones/subresources/settings/methods/edit/"><code>PATCH</code></a> request with <code>rocket_loader</code> as the setting name in the URI path, and the <code>value</code> parameter set to <code>&quot;on&quot;</code> or <code>&quot;off&quot;</code>.</p>
<p>If you have a <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="content security policy (CSP)">Content Security Policy (CSP)</span> in place for your domain, you will need to <a href="/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/content-security-policies/#product-requirements">update your headers</a> to support Rocket Loader.</p>

</section>
