<p>To enable SSL/TLS recommendations in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>SSL/TLS Overview</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>SSL/TLS Recommender</strong>, switch the toggle to <strong>On</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To adjust your SSL/TLS Recommender enrollment with the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/ssl/subresources/recommendations/methods/get/"><code>PATCH</code></a> request with the <code>enabled</code> parameter set to your desired setting (<code>true</code> or <code>false</code>).</p>
