<p>To add a CAA record in the dashboard,</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>DNS Records</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select <strong>Add record</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Type</strong>, select <strong>CAA</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Name</strong>, type your domain.</li>
<li>Choose a <strong>Tag</strong>, which specifies the behavior associated with the record.</li>
<li>For <strong>CA domain name</strong>, enter the CA name.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
<li>Repeat for each CA associated with your domain.</li>
</ol>
