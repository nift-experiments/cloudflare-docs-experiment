<p>To enable TLS 1.3 in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Edge Certificates</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>TLS 1.3</strong>, switch the toggle to <strong>On</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To adjust your TLS 1.3 settings with the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/zones/subresources/settings/methods/edit/"><code>PATCH</code></a> request with <code>tls_1_3</code> as the setting name in the URI path, and set the <code>value</code> parameter to your desired setting (<code>&quot;on&quot;</code>, <code>&quot;zrt&quot;</code> or <code>&quot;off&quot;</code>). <code>zrt</code> refers to <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-0-rtt/">Zero Round Trip Time Resumption (0-RTT)</a>.</p>

</section>
