
- Certificates that Cloudflare did not issue can still trigger CT Monitoring emails. This includes certificates issued by services outside Cloudflare, as well as custom certificates you upload yourself.
- If your domain is included in a shared certificate, you may receive notifications for domains or subdomains that do not belong to you but are included as <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="Subject Alternative Names (SANs)">subject alternative names (SANs)</span> together with your domain. You can use a tool like [Certificate Search](https://crt.sh/) to gather more information in such cases.
- CT Monitoring does not detect phishing attempts. For example, for `cloudflare.com`, an alert would not trigger if a certificate was issued for `cloudf1are.com` or `cloud-flare.com`.
