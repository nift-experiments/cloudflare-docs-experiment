<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To enable Total TLS in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Edge Certificates</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>Total TLS</strong>, switch the toggle to <strong>On</strong> and - if desired - choose an issuing <strong>Certificate Authority</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To enable Total TLS with the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/acm/subresources/total_tls/methods/create/"><code>PATCH</code></a> request with the <code>enabled</code> parameter set to your desired setting (<code>true</code> or <code>false</code>).</p>
<p>You can also specify a desired certificate authority by adding a value to the <code>certificate_authority</code> parameter.</p>

</section>
