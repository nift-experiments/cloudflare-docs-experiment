<p>To reset to the default cipher suites at zone level, use the <a href="/api/resources/zones/subresources/settings/methods/edit/">Edit zone setting</a> endpoint, specifying <code>ciphers</code> as the setting name in the URL, and send an empty array in the <code>value</code> field.</p>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request PATCH \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/{zone_id}/settings/{setting_id} \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;value&quot;: []
}&#x27;</code></pre>
