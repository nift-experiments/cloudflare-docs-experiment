<ol>

</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Choose an encryption mode.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To adjust your encryption mode with the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/zones/subresources/settings/methods/edit/"><code>PATCH</code></a> request with <code>ssl</code> as the setting name in the URI path, and the <code>value</code> parameter set to your desired setting (<code>off</code>, <code>flexible</code>, <code>full</code>, <code>strict</code>, or <code>origin_pull</code>).</p>
