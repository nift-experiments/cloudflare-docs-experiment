

If your tunnel token is compromised, immediately [rotate the token](#rotate-a-token), then force-disconnect all existing connections:



<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request DELETE \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/cfd_tunnel/{tunnel_id}/connections \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot;</code></pre>



Then reinstall the `cloudflared` service on all replicas using the new token.
