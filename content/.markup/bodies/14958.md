


When deploying `cloudflared` in a [FedRAMP High](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-for-government/) environment, `cloudflared` automatically routes to FedRAMP data centers based on the <a href="/tunnel/reference/tunnel-tokens/">tunnel token</a>. Ensure your firewall allows outbound connections to these FedRAMP-specific destinations on port `7844` (TCP/UDP).
