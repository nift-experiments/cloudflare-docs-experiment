Enterprise customers with [Bot Management](/bots/plans/bm-subscription/) can reference the `cf.bot_management.score` field in custom rule expressions for granular control over bot traffic. Bot Management assigns a score from 1 to 99 to each request, where lower scores indicate more automated behavior. Refer to [Bot scores](/bots/concepts/bot-score/) and [Bot Management variables](/bots/reference/bot-management-variables/) for details.
