Only turn on HSTS after your HTTPS configuration is fully working and tested. If you remove HTTPS before disabling HSTS or before waiting for the duration of the original **Max Age Header**, your website becomes inaccessible to visitors for the Max Age Header duration or until you re-enable HTTPS. Refer to [HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS)](/ssl/edge-certificates/additional-options/http-strict-transport-security/) before configuring.
