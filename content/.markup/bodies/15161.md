If your site shows a [526 error](/support/troubleshooting/http-status-codes/cloudflare-5xx-errors/error-526/) after changing the encryption mode, your origin certificate may not meet the requirements for Full (strict). The certificate must be unexpired, issued by a trusted CA or Cloudflare Origin CA, and contain a Common Name (CN) or Subject Alternative Name (SAN) that matches your hostname. For redirect loop issues, refer to [ERR_TOO_MANY_REDIRECTS](/ssl/troubleshooting/too-many-redirects/).
