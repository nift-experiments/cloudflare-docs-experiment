

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Edge Certificates</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>
<p>For <strong>HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS)</strong>, select <strong>Enable HSTS</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Read the dialog and select <strong>I understand</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select <strong>Next</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Configure the HSTS settings:</p>
</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Max Age Header</strong>: Choose a duration (start with 6 months, increase to 12 months after confirming stability).</li>
<li><strong>Apply HSTS policy to subdomains (includeSubDomains)</strong>: Turn on if all your subdomains support HTTPS. Subdomains without HTTPS become inaccessible when this is enabled.</li>
<li><strong>Preload</strong>: Turn on only after setting Max Age Header to 12 months. Preload inclusion prevents downgrade attacks on the first visit. Submit your domain at <a href="https://hstspreload.org">hstspreload.org</a> after turning on preload.</li>
<li><strong>No-Sniff Header</strong>: Sends the <code>X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff</code> header. Turn on to prevent browsers from MIME-type sniffing.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="6">
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</div>
