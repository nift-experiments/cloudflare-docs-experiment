

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the SSL/TLS **Overview** page.

<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>

2. Verify that your [SSL/TLS encryption mode](/ssl/origin-configuration/ssl-modes/off/) is not set to **Off**. The Always Use HTTPS option is not visible when encryption is off.

3. Go to the **Edge Certificates** page.

<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>

4. Turn on **Always Use HTTPS**.
