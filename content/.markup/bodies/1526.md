<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To enable <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/traffic">Argo Smart Routing</a> in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Argo Smart Routing</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>Argo Smart Routing</strong>, switch the toggle to <strong>On</strong>.</li>
<li>Provide your billing information.</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li>
<p>If you do not have a <a href="/billing/get-started/create-billing-profile/">billing profile</a>, enter your billing information.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you have a billing profile, confirm your billing information.</p>
</li>
</ul>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To enable or disable Argo Smart Routing with the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/argo/subresources/smart_routing/methods/edit/"><code>PATCH</code></a> request with the <code>value</code> parameter set to your desired setting (<code>&quot;on&quot;</code> or <code>&quot;off&quot;</code>).</p>
<p>You will need to already have a <a href="/billing/get-started/create-billing-profile/">billing profile</a> on your account to enable Argo Smart Routing.</p>

</section>
