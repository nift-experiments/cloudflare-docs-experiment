
Currently, you cannot skip [Bot Fight Mode](/bots/get-started/bot-fight-mode/), only Super Bot Fight Mode.

Skipping a phase does not skip security products that run outside the Ruleset Engine, such as [Browser Integrity Check](/waf/tools/browser-integrity-check/) or [Zone Lockdown](/waf/tools/zone-lockdown/). To skip those products, use the [Skip products](#skip-products) option instead.
