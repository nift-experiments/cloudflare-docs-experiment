

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the Security <strong>Settings</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>(Optional) Filter by <strong>Detection tools</strong>.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Malicious uploads detection</strong> &gt; <strong>Configurations</strong>, select the edit icon.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add content location</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Content location</strong>, enter your custom scan expression. For example:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-txt">lookup_json_string(http.request.body.raw, &quot;file&quot;)
</code></pre>
<ol start="6">
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>
