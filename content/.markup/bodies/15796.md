<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Web3</strong> page.</li>

<ol start="2">
<li>On a specific gateway, click the dropdown then <strong>Remove</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Delete hostname</strong>.</li>
</ol>
