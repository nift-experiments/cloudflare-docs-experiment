Ensure you are running `cloudflared` version 2025.7.0 or later (latest version recommended) to ensure proper DNS resolution and connectivity. Older versions are not supported.

Workers VPC also requires Cloudflare Tunnel to connect using the [QUIC transport protocol](/cloudflare-one/networks/connectors/cloudflare-tunnel/configure-tunnels/run-parameters/#protocol) using `auto` or `quic`. Ensure outbound UDP traffic on port 7844 is allowed through your firewall for QUIC connections.
