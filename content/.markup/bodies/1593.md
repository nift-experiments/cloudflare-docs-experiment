

You can also create gateways manually with a custom name and configuration through the dashboard or API.

<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol>
<li>Log into the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a> and select your account.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>AI</strong> &gt; <strong>AI Gateway</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Create Gateway</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter your <strong>Gateway name</strong>. Note: Gateway name has a 64 character limit.</li>
<li>In <strong>Workers AI Billing</strong>, choose how Workers AI requests through this gateway are billed:</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Standard billing</strong> charges your Cloudflare account at the end of each billing cycle.</li>
<li><strong>Unified billing</strong> deducts from your prepaid AI Gateway credit balance in real time.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="6">
<li>Select <strong>Create</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</ul>

</li>
</ol>
