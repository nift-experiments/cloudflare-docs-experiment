<aside class="nb-aside note">
<h3 class="nb-aside-title" id="body-1613-quality-of-ai-crawler-detection">Quality of AI crawler detection</h3>
<p>On the free plan, AI Crawl Control identifies AI crawlers based on their <a href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Reference/Headers/User-Agent">user agent strings</a>. This enables AI Crawl Control to detect well-known, self-identifying AI crawlers.</p>
<p>Upgrade your plan to enable a more thorough detection using Cloudflare's <a href="/bots/reference/bot-management-variables/#ruleset-engine-fields">Bot Management detection ID</a> field.</p>
</aside>
