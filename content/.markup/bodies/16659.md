

Dashboard path: **Workers & Pages** overview page > **Protect all Workers**.

![Workers & Pages overview showing the Protect all Workers card and the Workers application list.](/assets/upstream/images/workers/access/protect-all-workers-card.png)

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Workers &amp; Pages</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Find the <strong>Protect all Workers</strong> card.</li>
<li>If the card says <strong>Not enabled</strong>, select <strong>Enable Access</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>Previews only</strong> or <strong>All traffic</strong>.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Authentication policy</strong>, select an existing policy or configure one of the <a href="#policy-options">policy options</a>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Enable Access</strong>.</li>
<li>(Optional) Review the session duration.</li>
</ol>
<p><img src="/assets/upstream/images/workers/access/manage-access-all-workers.png" alt="Manage Access for all Workers dialog showing traffic scope and policy options." /></p>
<ol start="8">
<li>Select <strong>Apply Access</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</div>
