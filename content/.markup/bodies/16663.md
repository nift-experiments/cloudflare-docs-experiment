

Dashboard path: **Workers & Pages** > select your Worker > **Access**.

![Worker Access tab showing an unprotected Worker and the Protect this Worker behind Access button.](/assets/upstream/images/workers/access/worker-access-tab-unprotected.png)

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Workers &amp; Pages</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select your Worker from the application list.</li>
<li>Select the <strong>Access</strong> tab.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Protect this Worker behind Access</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>Previews only</strong> or <strong>All traffic</strong>.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Authentication policy</strong>, select an existing policy or configure one of the <a href="#policy-options">policy options</a>.</li>
<li>(Optional) Review the session duration.</li>
</ol>
<p><img src="/assets/upstream/images/workers/access/enable-access-one-worker.png" alt="Enable Access on one Worker dialog showing traffic scope and policy options." /></p>
<ol start="8">
<li>Select <strong>Apply Access</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</div>
