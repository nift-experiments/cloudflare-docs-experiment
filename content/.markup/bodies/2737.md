

For each AI crawler, you can choose to allow or block access.

<div class="nb-example"><h3 class="nb-component-title" id="body-2737-allow-access">Allow access</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Summary:</strong> You can allow an AI crawler to scrape your content.</li>
<li><strong>When to use:</strong> Allow AI crawlers that offer services which provide value through citations, referrals, or existing agreements.</li>
<li><strong>Implementation:</strong> From the <strong>Actions</strong> column, select <strong>Allow</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>Note that you can still choose to <a href="/ai-crawl-control/features/manage-ai-crawlers/#take-action-for-each-ai-crawler">Enforce <code>robots.txt</code></a>.</p>

</div>

<div class="nb-example"><h3 class="nb-component-title" id="body-2737-block-access">Block access</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Summary:</strong> You can block an AI crawler to completely stop the AI crawler from scraping your webpage.</li>
<li><strong>When to use:</strong> Block AI crawlers when their behavior do not align with your content strategy, or violate your policies.</li>
<li><strong>Implementation:</strong> From the <strong>Actions</strong> column, select <strong>Block</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>Note that you can configure the response that gets returned when blocking an AI crawler. Refer to <a href="/ai-crawl-control/features/manage-ai-crawlers/#configure-block-response">Configure block response</a>.</p>

</div>
