<p>To delete an AI Gateway in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log into the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a> and select your account.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>AI</strong> &gt; <strong>AI Gateway</strong>.</li>
<li>Select your gateway from the list of available options.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Delete Gateway</strong>, select <strong>Delete</strong> (and confirm your deletion).</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To delete an AI Gateway, send a <a href="/api/resources/ai_gateway/methods/delete/"><code>DELETE</code> request</a> to the Cloudflare API.</p>
