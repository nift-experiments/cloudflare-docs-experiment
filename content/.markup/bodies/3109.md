

Set the record to **Proxied**, not **DNS only**. A proxied record routes traffic through your own zone before it reaches AI Search, so your zone settings apply first. This is what makes [Cloudflare Access](/ai-search/configuration/retrieval/public-endpoint/cloudflare-access/), [WAF custom rules](/waf/custom-rules/), and [Bot Management](/bots/) usable in front of your public endpoint.
