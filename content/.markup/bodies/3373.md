

Cloudflare provides two tools:

1. **[Billable usage dashboard](/billing/manage/billable-usage/)** — shows daily usage-based costs per product with a chart and sortable table. Select **Manage Account** > **Billing** > **Billable Usage** in the dashboard.
2. **[Budget alerts](/billing/manage/budget-alerts/)** — sends an email when your total spend crosses a dollar threshold you define.

For optimization strategies, refer to [Optimize costs](/billing/manage/optimize-costs/).
