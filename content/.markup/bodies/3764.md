
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Address Maps</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select <strong>Create an address map</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the scope of the address map.</li>
<li>Add the zones and IP addresses that you want to map.</li>
<li>Name your address map.</li>
<li>Review the information and select <strong>Save and Deploy</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>Use the <a href="/api/resources/addressing/subresources/address_maps/methods/create/">Create Address Map</a> endpoint.</p>
<p>Make sure you have the correct Key/Token and permissions.</p>
