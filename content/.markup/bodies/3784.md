<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Address Maps</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Go to your address map and select <strong>Review</strong>.</li>
<li>Edit your address map.</li>
<li>Review the information and select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
<p>You can also enable, disable, and delete address maps. This will likely change the IP addresses used for your zones.</p>
</aside>
