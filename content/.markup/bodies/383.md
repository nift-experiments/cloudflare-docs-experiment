These pages document the current stable `@cloudflare/sandbox` package. The next major release is **Sandbox SDK 1.0**, available as a preview on `@cloudflare/sandbox@next`.

We recommend starting new projects on the preview, and migrating existing apps when you can, so you are ready when 1.0 becomes the stable release. Refer to the [1.0 preview](/sandbox/1-0-preview/) section for install, concepts, API reference, and migration.
