
[Single-file purge](/cache/how-to/purge-cache/purge-by-single-file/) may not work as expected if your Cache Rule sets a [custom cache key](/cache/how-to/cache-keys/) (because the dashboard cannot send the headers or cookies that are part of the key), or if your rule expression matches only `GET` requests (because purge requests use a different HTTP method internally).

For more information and alternatives, refer to [Purge by single-file limitations](/cache/how-to/purge-cache/purge-by-single-file/#limitations-and-alternatives).
