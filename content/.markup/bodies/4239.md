<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/networking-insights/health">Network health</a>.</li>
<li>Select the <strong>Diagnostics</strong> tab &gt; <strong>Buckets</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add a bucket</strong>.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Bucket configuration</strong>, select a bucket service and select <strong>Next</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter the information related to your bucket for your service provider.</li>
<li>When you are done, select <strong>Next</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>The <strong>Prove ownership</strong> step of the <strong>Bucket configuration</strong> displays.</p>
