<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic policies</strong> &gt; <strong>Resolver policies</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add a policy</strong>.</li>
<li>Create an expression for your desired traffic. For example, you can resolve a hostname for an internal service:</li>
</ol>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Selector</th>
<th>Operator</th>
<th>Value</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Host</td>
<td>in</td>
<td><code>internal.example.com</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>Make sure your destination is not subject to <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/configure/route-traffic/local-domains/#manage-local-domains">Local Domain Fallback</a>.</p>
<ol start="4">
<li>In <strong>Select DNS resolver</strong>, choose <em>Configure custom DNS resolvers</em>.</li>
<li>Enter the IP addresses of your custom DNS resolver. As you enter an IP address, Gateway will search through your <a href="/cloudflare-one/networks/connectors/cloudflare-tunnel/private-net/cloudflared/tunnel-virtual-networks/">virtual networks</a> configured in Zero Trust.</li>
<li>In <strong>Network</strong>, choose whether to route queries publicly (to the Internet) or privately (to a private network service).</li>
<li>(Optional) Enter a custom port for each IP address.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Create policy</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>Custom resolvers are saved to your account for future use. You can add up to 10 IPv4 and 10 IPv6 addresses to a policy.</p>

<ul>
<li><code>Zero Trust Write</code></li>
</ul>
<ol start="2">
<li>Create a resolver policy using the <a href="https://registry.terraform.io/providers/cloudflare/cloudflare/latest/docs/resources/zero_trust_gateway_policy"><code>cloudflare_zero_trust_gateway_policy</code></a> resource:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-tf">resource &quot;cloudflare_zero_trust_gateway_policy&quot; &quot;resolver_policy&quot; {
	name        = &quot;Example resolver policy&quot;

	description = &quot;TERRAFORM MANAGED resolver policy&quot;

	identity    = &quot;identity.email in {\&quot;jdoe@example.com\&quot;}&quot;

}
