<div class="nb-steps">
<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Access controls</strong> &gt; <strong>Service credentials</strong> &gt; <strong>Service Tokens</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Locate the token and select the three dots &gt; <strong>Rotate secret</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Keep the current secret valid for</strong>, choose when Access should revoke the current secret. Available grace periods range from one hour to 30 days. To revoke it when you rotate, select <em>Revoke immediately</em>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Rotate</strong>.</li>
<li>Copy the new Client Secret and update your services before the grace period ends.</li>
</ol>
</div>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>Make a <code>POST</code> request to the <a href="/api/resources/zero_trust/subresources/access/subresources/service_tokens/methods/rotate/">Rotate a service token</a> endpoint. Set <code>previous_client_secret_expires_at</code> to an RFC 3339 timestamp when the previous secret should expire:</p>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/access/service_tokens/{service_token_id}/rotate \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;previous_client_secret_expires_at&quot;: &quot;2030-01-01T00:00:00Z&quot;
}&#x27;</code></pre>
<p>To revoke the previous secret immediately, omit <code>previous_client_secret_expires_at</code> from the request.</p>
