

<div class="nb-steps">

<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Access controls</strong> &gt; <strong>Service credentials</strong> &gt; <strong>Service Tokens</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Locate the token and select the three dots.</li>
<li>To stop the token from authenticating, select <strong>Disable token</strong> &gt; <strong>Disable</strong>.</li>
<li>To restore authentication, select <strong>Enable token</strong> &gt; <strong>Enable</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</div>
