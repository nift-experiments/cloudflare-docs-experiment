The `Cf-Client-Cert-Der-Base64` and `Cf-Client-Cert-Sha256` headers are a Cloudflare-proprietary mechanism. For a standardized approach, use [RFC 9440 `Client-Cert` and `Client-Cert-Chain` headers](/ssl/client-certificates/forward-a-client-certificate/#add-client-cert-and-client-cert-chain-headers-rfc-9440).
