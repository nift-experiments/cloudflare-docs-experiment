

Send a `DELETE` request to remove a specific authenticator:



<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request DELETE \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/access/users/{user_id}/mfa_authenticators/{authenticator_id} \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot;</code></pre>



Parameters:

- `user_id` — The UUID of the user. You can find this in the user details under **Team & Resources** > **Users**.
- `authenticator_id` — The unique identifier for the authenticator.
