<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Integrations</strong> &gt; <strong>Identity providers</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add new identity provider</strong> and select <strong>SAML</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose a descriptive name for your identity provider.</li>
<li>Enter the <strong>Single Sign on URL</strong>, <strong>IdP Entity ID or Issuer URL</strong>, and <strong>Signing certificate</strong> obtained from your identity provider.</li>
<li>(Optional) To enable SCIM, refer to <a href="#synchronize-users-and-groups">Synchronize users and groups</a>.</li>
<li>(Optional) Under <strong>Optional configurations</strong>, configure <a href="#optional-configurations">additional SAML options</a>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>
