<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>
<p>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Integrations</strong> &gt; <strong>Identity providers</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Under <strong>Your identity providers</strong>, select <strong>Add new identity provider</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Choose <strong>OpenID Connect</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Name your identity provider and fill in the required fields with the information obtained from your identity provider.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>(Optional) Enable <a href="https://www.oauth.com/oauth2-servers/pkce/">Proof of Key Exchange (PKCE)</a> if the protocol is supported by your IdP. PKCE will be performed on all login attempts.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>(Optional) To enable SCIM, refer to <a href="#synchronize-users-and-groups">Synchronize users and groups</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>(Optional) Under <strong>Optional configurations</strong>, enter <a href="#oidc-claims">custom OIDC claims</a> that you wish to add to users' identity. This information will be available in the <a href="/cloudflare-one/access-controls/applications/http-apps/authorization-cookie/application-token/#user-identity">user identity endpoint</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p>Make a <code>POST</code> request to the <a href="/api/resources/zero_trust/subresources/identity_providers/methods/create/">Identity Providers</a> endpoint:</p>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/access/identity_providers \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;name&quot;: &quot;Generic OIDC example&quot;,
&quot;type&quot;: &quot;oidc&quot;,
&quot;config&quot;: {
  &quot;client_id&quot;: &quot;&lt;your client id&gt;&quot;,
  &quot;client_secret&quot;: &quot;&lt;your client secret&gt;&quot;,
  &quot;auth_url&quot;: &quot;https://accounts.google.com/o/oauth2/auth&quot;,
  &quot;token_url&quot;: &quot;https://accounts.google.com/o/oauth2/token&quot;,
  &quot;certs_url&quot;: &quot;https://www.googleapis.com/oauth2/v3/certs&quot;,
  &quot;pkce_enabled&quot;: false,
  &quot;email_claim_name&quot;: &quot;email&quot;,
  &quot;claims&quot;: [
