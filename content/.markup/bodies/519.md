<p>Enable the catalog on your R2 bucket:</p>
<pre><code class="language-bash">npx wrangler r2 bucket catalog enable pipelines-tutorial
</code></pre>
<p>When you run this command, take note of the &quot;Warehouse&quot; and &quot;Catalog URI&quot;. You will need these later.</p>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
