<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to <strong>Networking</strong> &gt; <strong>Mesh</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select your Mesh node.</li>
<li>Go to the <strong>Routes</strong> tab.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add route</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter the private CIDR you want to route through this node (for example, <code>10.0.0.0/24</code>).</li>
<li>(Optionally) add a description for the route.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add route</strong>.</li>
</ol>
