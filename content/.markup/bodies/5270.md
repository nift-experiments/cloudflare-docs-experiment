<p>Open PowerShell and run the following command:</p>
<pre><code class="language-powershell">PS C:\Users\JohnDoe&gt; Test-NetConnection test.example.com -port 443
</code></pre>
<p>If the output shows <code>TcpTestSucceeded : False</code>, check your infrastructure for firewalls, load balancers, or other network devices that may be interfering with the connection between <code>cloudflared</code> and the application server.</p>
