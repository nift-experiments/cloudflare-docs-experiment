
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Version 2026.2+">

<ol>
<li>Open the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Home</strong>.</li>
<li>In the <strong>VNET</strong> dropdown, choose the virtual network you want to connect to (for example, <code>staging-vnet</code>).</li>
</ol>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Version 2026.1 and earlier">

<ol>
<li>Open the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic and DNS mode</strong> &gt; <strong>Virtual Networks</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the virtual network you want to connect to, for example <code>staging-vnet</code>.</li>
</ol>

</section>
