
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic policies</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic settings</strong> &gt; <strong>SSH log encryption public key</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Remove</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Remove key</strong> to confirm.</li>
</ol>
<p>Cloudflare will stop logging SSH commands to your targets.</p>

</section>
