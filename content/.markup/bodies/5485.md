<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>Traffic policies</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic settings</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Proxy and inspection</strong>, turn on <strong>Allow Secure Web Gateway to proxy traffic</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>TCP</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>UDP</strong> (required to proxy traffic to internal DNS resolvers).</li>
<li>(Recommended) To proxy traffic for diagnostic tools such as <code>ping</code> and <code>traceroute</code>, select <strong>ICMP</strong>. You may also need to <a href="/cloudflare-one/traffic-policies/proxy/#icmp">update your system</a> to allow ICMP traffic through <code>cloudflared</code>.</li>
</ol>
