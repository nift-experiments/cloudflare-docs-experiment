

**Setup time:** 30-45 minutes

**Required products:** [Cloudflare Tunnel](/cloudflare-one/networks/connectors/cloudflare-tunnel/) (`cloudflared` on server), [Cloudflare One Client](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/) or [Cloudflare WAN](/cloudflare-wan/) (client on-ramp), [Gateway](/cloudflare-one/traffic-policies/)
