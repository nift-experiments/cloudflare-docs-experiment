<div class="nb-steps">
<ol>
<li>If not already logged in, run:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-bash">npx wrangler login
</code></pre>
<ol start="2">
<li>Create an R2 bucket:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-bash">npx wrangler r2 bucket create r2-data-catalog-tutorial
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Enable the catalog on your bucket:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-bash">npx wrangler r2 bucket catalog enable r2-data-catalog-tutorial
</code></pre>
<p>When you run this command, take note of the <strong>Warehouse</strong> and <strong>Catalog URI</strong>. You will need these later.</p>
</div>

</section>
