
1. Create a directory where our notebook will be stored:

<pre><code class="language-bash">mkdir r2-data-catalog-notebook
</code></pre>


2. Change into our new directory:

<pre><code class="language-bash">cd r2-data-catalog-notebook
</code></pre>


3. Initialize a new uv project (this creates a `.venv` and a `pyproject.toml`):

<pre><code>uv init
</code></pre>


4. Add marimo and required dependencies:

<pre><code class="language-py">uv add marimo pyiceberg pyarrow pandas
</code></pre>
