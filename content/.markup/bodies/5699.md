<p>You can adjust the health check frequency by updating your <a href="/api/resources/magic_transit/subresources/gre_tunnels/methods/update/">GRE</a>, <a href="/api/resources/magic_transit/subresources/ipsec_tunnels/methods/update/">IPsec</a>, or <a href="/api/resources/magic_transit/subresources/cf_interconnects/methods/update/">CNI</a> tunnels.</p>
<p>The following example adjusts tunnel health check frequency to <code>low</code>. Note that this command applies to GRE, IPsec and CNI tunnels:</p>
<pre><code class="language-bash">curl --request PUT --url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/magic/ipsec_tunnels/{ipsec_tunnel_id}</code></pre>
