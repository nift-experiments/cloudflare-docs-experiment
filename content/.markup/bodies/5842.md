

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In <a href="https://one.dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare One</a>, go to <strong>Networks</strong> &gt; <strong>Resolvers &amp; Proxies</strong> &gt; <strong>Proxy endpoints</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the proxy endpoint. Select <strong>Edit</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Proxy Endpoint</strong>, copy the domain.</li>
</ol>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<ol>
<li>Use the <a href="/api/resources/zero_trust/subresources/gateway/subresources/proxy_endpoints/methods/list/">List proxy endpoints</a> operation to get a list of your proxy endpoints and their details. For example:</li>
</ol>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request GET \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/gateway/proxy_endpoints \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot;</code></pre>
