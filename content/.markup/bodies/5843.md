

1. Open a terminal.
2. Run `dig` on your proxy endpoint's A records to get its IPv4 addresses. For example:

<pre><code class="language-bash">dig A example.cloudflare-gateway.com +short
</code></pre>


<pre><code class="language-txt">162.159.36.5
162.159.36.20
</code></pre>


3. Run `dig` on your proxy endpoint's AAAA records to get its IPv6 addresses. For example:

<pre><code class="language-bash">dig AAAA example.cloudflare-gateway.com +short
</code></pre>


<pre><code class="language-txt">2606:4700:54::a29f:2407
2606:4700:5c::a29f:2e07
</code></pre>
