

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="macOS and Linux">

<ol>
<li>Open a terminal.</li>
<li>Run <code>dig</code> on your proxy endpoint's A records to get its IPv4 addresses. For example:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-bash">dig A example.cloudflare-gateway.com +short
</code></pre>
<pre><code class="language-txt">162.159.36.5
162.159.36.20
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Run <code>dig</code> on your proxy endpoint's AAAA records to get its IPv6 addresses. For example:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-bash">dig AAAA example.cloudflare-gateway.com +short
</code></pre>
<pre><code class="language-txt">2606:4700:54::a29f:2407
2606:4700:5c::a29f:2e07
</code></pre>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Windows">

<ol>
<li>Open a PowerShell terminal.</li>
<li>Run <code>Resolve-DnsName</code> on your proxy endpoint's A records. Your proxy endpoint's IPv4 addresses will appear under <code>IPAddress</code>. For example:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-powershell">Resolve-DnsName -Name example.cloudflare-gateway.com -Type A
</code></pre>
<pre><code class="language-txt">Name                                           Type   TTL   Section    IPAddress
&#45;---                                           ----   ---   -------    ---------
example.cloudflare-gateway.com                 A      300   Answer     162.159.36.5
example.cloudflare-gateway.com                 A      300   Answer     162.159.36.20
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Run <code>Resolve-DnsName</code> on your proxy endpoint's AAAA records. Your proxy endpoint's IPv6 addresses will appear under <code>IPAddress</code>. For example:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-powershell">Resolve-DnsName -Name example.cloudflare-gateway.com -Type AAAA
</code></pre>
<pre><code class="language-txt">Name                                           Type   TTL   Section    IPAddress
&#45;---                                           ----   ---   -------    ---------
example.cloudflare-gateway.com                 AAAA   300   Answer     2606:4700:5c::a29f:2e07
example.cloudflare-gateway.com                 AAAA   300   Answer     2606:4700:54::a29f:2407
</code></pre>

</section>
