
Gateway does not terminate an [active session](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/configure/client-sessions/#configure-warp-sessions-in-gateway) even if a subsequent posture check fails during that session. Gateway only evaluates posture checks at the beginning of a session, and ongoing sessions will remain uninterrupted.

For example, if you establish an SSH session based on a successful posture check, but a posture requirement fails after the session has started, the session will remain active.
