
If a user re-authenticates while the device registration is revoked, a new device registration will be created for the user. For long-term, permanent denial of access, you should [remove the user from your device enrollment policies or your IdP](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/device-registration/#remove-user-access) and [remove service token access](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/device-registration/#remove-service-token-access).
