
Deleting or revoking a device registration is not permanent without [removing user access](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/device-registration/#remove-user-access) and [removing service token access](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/device-registration/#remove-service-token-access).

If the user or service token can successfully re-authenticate, a new device registration will be automatically created following a deletion or revocation.
