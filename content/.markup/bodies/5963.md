

To get a list of all device registrations, including active and revoked registrations:



<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request GET \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/devices/registrations \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot;</code></pre>
