<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To review how many device registrations are associated with a device:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a> and go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Teams &amp; Resources</strong> &gt; <strong>Devices</strong>.</li>
<li>Select a device and select <strong>View details</strong>.</li>
<li>Scroll down to <strong>Users</strong> and review users who enrolled on this device.</li>
</ol>
<p>To review a device registration's status:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Teams &amp; Resources</strong> &gt; <strong>Devices</strong>.</li>
<li>Select the device and select <strong>View details</strong>.</li>
<li>Scroll down to <strong>Users</strong> and find the user associated with the device.</li>
<li>Review the status (such as <code>Active</code> or <code>Revoked</code>) of the device registration under <strong>Status</strong>.</li>
</ol>
