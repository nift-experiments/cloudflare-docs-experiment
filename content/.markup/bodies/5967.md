<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To delete a device registration:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Teams &amp; Resources</strong> &gt; <strong>Devices</strong>.</li>
<li>Select the device &gt; <strong>View details</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Users</strong> and mark the checkbox next to the device registration you want to delete.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Action</strong> &gt; <em>Delete access</em>.</li>
</ol>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To delete a single device registration using the <a href="/api/resources/zero_trust/subresources/devices/subresources/registrations/methods/delete/">API</a>:</p>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request DELETE \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/devices/registrations/{registration_id} \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot;</code></pre>
<p>To bulk delete multiple device registrations:</p>
<pre><code class="language-curl">curl &quot;https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/device/registrations/registrations?id=reg_id_1&amp;id=reg_id_2&amp;id=reg_id_3&quot; \
	--request DELETE\
	--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot;
</code></pre>

</section>
