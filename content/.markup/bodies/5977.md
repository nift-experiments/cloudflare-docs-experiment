
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="macOS and Linux">

<ol>
<li><a href="https://wiki.openssl.org/index.php/Compilation_and_Installation">Install OpenSSL</a>.</li>
<li><a href="#download-a-cloudflare-root-certificate">Download a Cloudflare certificate</a> in <code>.pem</code> format.</li>
<li>In a terminal, convert the certificate to DER format with the <code>.cer</code> file type:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">openssl x509 -inform PEM -in ~/Downloads/certificate.pem -outform DER -out ~/Downloads/certificate.cer
</code></pre>
