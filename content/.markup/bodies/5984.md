
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Debian-based">

<p>The following procedure applies to Debian-based systems, such as Debian, Ubuntu, and Kali Linux.</p>
<ol>
<li><a href="#download-a-cloudflare-root-certificate">Download a Cloudflare certificate</a> in <code>.pem</code> format.</li>
<li>Install the <code>ca-certificates</code> package.</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">sudo apt-get install ca-certificates
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Copy the certificate to the system, changing the file extension to <code>.crt</code>.</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">sudo cp certificate.pem /usr/share/ca-certificates/certificate.crt
</code></pre>
<ol start="4">
<li>Import the certificate.</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">sudo dpkg-reconfigure ca-certificates
</code></pre>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Red Hat-based">

<p>The following procedure applies to Red Hat-based systems, such as CentOS and Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL).</p>
<ol>
<li><a href="#download-a-cloudflare-root-certificate">Download a Cloudflare certificate</a> in both <code>.crt</code> and <code>.pem</code> format.</li>
<li>Install the <code>ca-certificates</code> package.</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">sudo dnf install ca-certificates
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Copy both certificates to the trust store.</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">sudo cp certificate.crt certificate.pem /etc/pki/ca-trust/source/anchors
</code></pre>
<ol start="4">
<li>Import the certificate.</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">sudo update-ca-trust
</code></pre>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="NixOS">

<p>NixOS does not use the system certificate store for self updating and instead relies on the certificates found in <code>~/.nix-profile/etc/ssl/certs</code> or provided by <code>NIX_SSL_CERT_FILE</code> at runtime.</p>

</section>
