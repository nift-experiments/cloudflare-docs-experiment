<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="VPN and apps">

<ol>
<li>
<p><a href="#download-the-cloudflare-root-certificate">Download a Cloudflare certificate</a> in <code>.crt</code> format.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Apps</strong> &gt; <strong>Google Play Store</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select <strong>Manage Android preferences</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Go to <strong>Security &amp; location</strong> &gt; <strong>Credentials</strong> &gt; <strong>Install from SD card</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>In the file open dialog, choose the <code>certificate.crt</code> file you downloaded. Select <strong>Open</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Enter a name to identify the certificate. Ensure <strong>Credential use</strong> is set to <em>VPN and apps</em>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select <strong>OK</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="CA certificate">

<ol>
<li>
<p><a href="#download-the-cloudflare-root-certificate">Download a Cloudflare certificate</a> in <code>.crt</code> format.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Apps</strong> &gt; <strong>Google Play Store</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select <strong>Manage Android preferences</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Go to <strong>Security &amp; location</strong> &gt; <strong>Credentials</strong> &gt; <strong>Install a certificate &gt; CA certificate</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>When prompted with a privacy warning, select <strong>Install anyway</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>In the file open dialog, choose the <code>certificate.crt</code> file you downloaded. Select <strong>Open</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To verify the certificate is installed and trusted, go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Apps</strong> &gt; <strong>Google Play Store</strong> &gt; <strong>Manage Android Preferences</strong> &gt; <strong>Security</strong> &gt; <strong>Credentials</strong> &gt; <strong>Trusted credentials</strong> &gt; <strong>User</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>

</section>
