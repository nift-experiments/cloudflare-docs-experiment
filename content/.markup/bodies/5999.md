
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Windows">

<ol>
<li><a href="#download-a-cloudflare-root-certificate">Download a Cloudflare certificate</a> in <code>.pem</code> format.</li>
<li>Set the <code>CARGO_HTTP_CAINFO</code> environment variable to point to the certificate. In PowerShell:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-powershell">[System.Environment]::SetEnvironmentVariable(&#x27;CARGO_HTTP_CAINFO&#x27;, &quot;$HOME\Downloads\certificate.pem&quot;, &#x27;User&#x27;)
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Restart your terminal.</li>
</ol>
<p>Alternatively, you can configure this in your Cargo configuration file at <code>%USERPROFILE%\.cargo\config.toml</code>:</p>
<pre><code class="language-toml">[http]
cainfo = &quot;C:\\Users\\&lt;username&gt;\\Downloads\\certificate.pem&quot;
</code></pre>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="macOS and Linux">

<ol>
<li><a href="#download-a-cloudflare-root-certificate">Download a Cloudflare certificate</a> in <code>.pem</code> format.</li>
<li>Set the <code>CARGO_HTTP_CAINFO</code> environment variable by adding it to your shell's configuration file (such as <code>~/.zshrc</code> or <code>~/.bash_profile</code>):</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-sh">export CARGO_HTTP_CAINFO=&quot;$HOME/Downloads/certificate.pem&quot;
</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Restart your terminal.</li>
</ol>
<p>Alternatively, you can configure this in your Cargo configuration file at <code>~/.cargo/config.toml</code>:</p>
<pre><code class="language-toml">[http]
cainfo = &quot;/path/to/certificate.pem&quot;
</code></pre>

</section>
