
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="macOS and Linux">

<p>Add the following to your shell's configuration file (such as <code>~/.zshrc</code> or <code>~/.bash_profile</code>):</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">export REQUESTS_CA_BUNDLE=&quot;$HOME/Downloads/certificate.pem&quot;
</code></pre>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Windows">

<p>In PowerShell:</p>
<pre><code class="language-powershell">[System.Environment]::SetEnvironmentVariable('REQUESTS_CA_BUNDLE', &quot;$HOME\Downloads\certificate.pem&quot;, 'User')
</code></pre>

</section>
