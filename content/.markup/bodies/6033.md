<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic policies</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic settings</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Certificates</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Generate certificate</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose a duration of time before the certificate expires. Cloudflare recommends expiration after five years. Alternatively, choose <em>Custom</em> and enter a custom amount in days.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Generate certificate</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>Send a <code>POST</code> request to the <a href="/api/resources/zero_trust/subresources/gateway/subresources/certificates/methods/create/">Create Zero Trust certificate</a> endpoint.</p>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/gateway/certificates \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot;</code></pre>
<p>The API will respond with the ID and contents of the new certificate.</p>

</section>
