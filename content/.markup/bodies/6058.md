If the end user's [connection toggle](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/configure/settings/#lock-device-client-switch) is locked, they will need an [admin override code](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/configure/settings/#allow-admin-override-codes) to be able to disconnect.

[Resetting the encryption keys](#option-b-reset-the-encryption-keys) may be a faster solution.
