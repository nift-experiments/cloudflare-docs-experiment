<ol>
<li>Launch the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Profile</strong> &gt; <strong>Account information</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Re-Authenticate</strong>.</li>
<li>Complete the authentication steps required by your organization.</li>
<li>If this does not resolve the error, select <strong>Logout</strong> and then <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/deployment/manual-deployment/">re-enroll your device</a>. Logging out is only possible if <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/configure/settings/#allow-device-to-leave-organization">Allow device to leave organization</a> is enabled for your device.</li>
<li>If the issue persists, contact your administrator for assistance.</li>
</ol>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Version 2026.1 and earlier">

<ol start="7">
<li>Launch the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Select the gear icon and go to <strong>Preferences</strong> &gt; <strong>Account</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Re-Authenticate Session</strong>.</li>
<li>Complete the authentication steps required by your organization.</li>
<li>If this does not resolve the error, select <strong>Logout from Cloudflare Zero Trust</strong> and then log back in. Logging out is only possible if <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/configure/settings/#allow-device-to-leave-organization">Allow device to leave organization</a> is enabled for your device.</li>
<li>If the issue persists, contact your administrator for assistance.</li>
</ol>

</section>
