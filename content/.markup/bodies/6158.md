<p><a href="/cloudflare-one/access-controls/service-credentials/service-tokens/#create-a-service-token">Create a service token</a>.</p>

<li>
<p>Copy the token's <strong>Client ID</strong> and <strong>Client Secret</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Go to <strong>Access controls</strong> &gt; <strong>Policies</strong> and create the following policy:</p>
</li>
</ol>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Rule Action</th>
<th>Rule type</th>
<th>Selector</th>
<th>Value</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Service Auth</td>
<td>Include</td>
<td>Service Token</td>
<td><code>&lt;TOKEN-NAME&gt;</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<pre><code> Make sure to set **Action** to _Service Auth_ instead of _Allow_.
</code></pre>
<ol start="4">
<li>Add the Access policy to your <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/deployment/device-enrollment/#set-device-enrollment-permissions">device enrollment permissions</a>.</li>
<li>In your MDM <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/deployment/mdm-deployment/parameters/">deployment parameters</a>, add the following fields:</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><code>auth_client_id</code>: The <strong>Client ID</strong> of your service token.</li>
<li><code>auth_client_secret</code>: The <strong>Client Secret</strong> of your service token.</li>
</ul>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Terraform (v5)">

<ol>
<li>Add the following permissions to your <a href="https://registry.terraform.io/providers/cloudflare/cloudflare/latest/docs/resources/api_token"><code>cloudflare_api_token</code></a>:</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><code>Access: Apps and Policies Write</code></li>
<li><code>Access: Service Tokens Write</code></li>
</ul>
<ol start="2">
<li>
<p><a href="/cloudflare-one/access-controls/service-credentials/service-tokens/#create-a-service-token">Create a service token</a> and copy its <strong>Client ID</strong> and <strong>Client Secret</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Create the following Access policy:</p>
</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-tf">resource &quot;cloudflare_zero_trust_access_policy&quot; &quot;warp_enrollment_service_token&quot; {
	account_id     = var.cloudflare_account_id
	name           = &quot;Allow service token&quot;
	decision       = &quot;non_identity&quot;
	include = [
		{
			service_token = {
				token_id = cloudflare_zero_trust_access_service_token.example_service_token.id
			}
		}
	]
}
</code></pre>
<ol start="4">
<li>
<p>Add the policy to your <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/deployment/device-enrollment/#set-device-enrollment-permissions"><code>cloudflared_zero_trust_access_application</code> for the Cloudflare One Client</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>In your MDM <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/deployment/mdm-deployment/parameters/">deployment parameters</a>, add the following fields:</p>
</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><code>auth_client_id</code>: The <strong>Client ID</strong> of your service token.</li>
<li><code>auth_client_secret</code>: The <strong>Client Secret</strong> of your service token.</li>
</ul>
