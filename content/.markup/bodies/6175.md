<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Team &amp; Resources</strong> &gt; <strong>Devices</strong> &gt; <strong>Device profiles</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Managed networks</strong> and select <strong>Add new managed network</strong>.</li>
<li>Name your network location.</li>
<li>In <strong>Host and Port</strong>, enter the private IP address and port number of your <a href="#create-a-new-tls-endpoint">TLS endpoint</a> (for example, <code>192.168.185.198:3333</code>).</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
<p>We recommend using the private IP of your managed network endpoint and not a hostname to prevent issues related to DNS lookups resolving the incorrect IP.</p>
</aside>
<ol start="5">
<li>(Optional) In <strong>TLS Cert SHA-256</strong>, enter the <a href="#2-extract-the-sha-256-fingerprint">SHA-256 fingerprint</a> of the TLS certificate. This field is only needed for self-signed certificates. If a TLS fingerprint is not supplied, the Cloudflare One Client validates the certificate against the local certificate store and checks that it is signed by a public certificate authority.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>
