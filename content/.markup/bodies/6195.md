<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="macOS">

<p>On macOS, run <code>ifconfig</code>. When the Cloudflare One Client is turned on, you will see a <code>utun</code> interface with your device IP.</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">ifconfig
</code></pre>
<pre><code class="language-sh">&lt;redacted&gt;
