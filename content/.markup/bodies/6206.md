Ensure that traffic destined to your device IPs routes from your private network to Cloudflare Gateway. For example, if you are using [Cloudflare Mesh](/mesh/) connectivity, you must configure your [Split Tunnel settings](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/configure/route-traffic/split-tunnels/) so that traffic to your Mesh IPs routes through the tunnel.
