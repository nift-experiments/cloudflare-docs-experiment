

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Windows and macOS">

<ol>
<li>Open the Cloudflare One Client and go to <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Temporarily access local network resources</strong>, select <strong>Access resources</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<details class="nb-details"><summary>Version 2026.1 and earlier</summary><div class="nb-details-body">
<ol>
<li>Open the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Select the gear icon.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Access Local Network</strong>.</li>
</ol>
</div></details>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Linux">

<ol>
<li>Open a terminal window.</li>
<li>Run <code>warp-cli override local-network start</code>.</li>
</ol>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Android and ChromeOS">

<ol>
<li>Open the Cloudflare One Agent app.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Advanced</strong> &gt; <strong>Connection Options</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Access Local Network</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section>
