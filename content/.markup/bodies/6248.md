The Cloudflare One Client will automatically exclude the external endpoint IP address from the WARP tunnel. If a device profile uses [Split Tunnels](/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/configure/route-traffic/split-tunnels/) in **Include** mode, ensure that your Split Tunnel entries do not contain the external endpoint IP; otherwise the Cloudflare One Client will exclude the entire Split Tunnel entry from the tunnel.
