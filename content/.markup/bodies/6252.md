<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Local certificate">

<p>To obtain the SHA-256 fingerprint of a local certificate:</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">openssl x509 -noout -fingerprint -sha256 -inform pem -in cert.pem | tr -d :
</code></pre>
<p>The output will look something like:</p>
<pre><code class="language-txt">SHA256 Fingerprint=DD4F4806C57A5BBAF1AA5B080F0541DA75DB468D0A1FE731310149500CCD8662
</code></pre>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Remote server">

<p>To test connectivity and obtain the SHA-256 fingerprint of a remote server:</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">openssl s_client -connect &lt;private-server-IP&gt;:443 &lt; /dev/null 2&gt; /dev/null | openssl x509 -noout -fingerprint -sha256 | tr -d :
</code></pre>
<p>The output will look something like:</p>
<pre><code class="language-txt">SHA256 Fingerprint=DD4F4806C57A5BBAF1AA5B080F0541DA75DB468D0A1FE731310149500CCD8662
</code></pre>

</section>
