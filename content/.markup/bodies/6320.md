<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Windows">

<ol>
<li>Open the <strong>Start</strong> menu and select <strong>Run</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter <code>certlm.msc</code>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Personal</strong> &gt; <strong>Certificates</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="macOS">

<ol>
<li>Open <strong>Keychain Access</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>System</strong> &gt; <strong>My Certificates</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Linux">

<p>Open a terminal window and run the following command:</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">$ certutil -L -d sql:/etc/pki/nssdb
</code></pre>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="iOS">

<p>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>General</strong> &gt; <strong>About</strong> &gt; <strong>Certificate Trust Settings</strong>.</p>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Android">

<p>The location of the client certificate may vary depending on the Android device.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Samsung</strong>: Go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Security</strong> &gt; <strong>Other security settings</strong> &gt; <strong>View security certificates</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Google Pixel</strong>: Go to <strong>Security</strong> &gt; <strong>Advanced settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Encryption &amp; credentials</strong> &gt; <strong>Credential storage</strong>.</li>
</ul>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="ChromeOS">

<p>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Apps</strong> &gt; <strong>Google Play Store</strong> &gt; <strong>Manage Android Preferences</strong> &gt; <strong>Security</strong> &gt; <strong>Credentials</strong>.</p>

</section>
