<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Version 2026.1 and earlier">

<ol>
<li>Open the Cloudflare One Client.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> (gear icon) <strong>Preferences</strong> &gt; <strong>General</strong>.</li>
<li>Note the <strong>Public IP</strong>. This is the same address that will appear as the Source IP in your network logs.</li>
</ol>
