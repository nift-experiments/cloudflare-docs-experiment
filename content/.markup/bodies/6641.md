
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic policies</strong> &gt; <strong>Firewall policies</strong> &gt; <strong>DNS</strong>.</li>
<li>Create a new DNS policy or choose an existing policy and select <strong>Edit</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Apply durations and schedules</strong>, turn on <strong>Policy schedule</strong>.</li>
<li>(Optional) In <strong>Time Zone</strong>, choose a time zone to apply the policy based on the time zone you select, regardless of the user's location. By default, Gateway will use the end user's time zone to apply the policy based on the local time of the user making the DNS query.</li>
<li>In <strong>Schedule template</strong>, choose a preset schedule, or choose <em>Custom schedule</em> to define a custom schedule. You can choose up to three non-overlapping time ranges of 15 minute intervals.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save policy</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To schedule a policy with the API, use the <a href="/api/resources/zero_trust/subresources/gateway/subresources/rules/methods/create/">Create a Zero Trust Gateway rule endpoint</a> with the <code>schedule</code> parameter set to your desired days of the week, times of day, and an optional time zone. For example:</p>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/gateway/rules \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;action&quot;: &quot;block&quot;,
&quot;name&quot;: &quot;Block gambling sites on weekends&quot;,
&quot;traffic&quot;: &quot;any(dns.content_category[*] in {\&quot;Gambling\&quot;})&quot;,
&quot;schedule&quot;: {
  &quot;sat&quot;: &quot;08:00-17:00&quot;,
  &quot;sun&quot;: &quot;08:00-17:00&quot;,
  &quot;timezone&quot;: &quot;Europe/Paris&quot;
}
}&#x27;</code></pre>
