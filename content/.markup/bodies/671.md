<p>To change the <strong>Pseudo IPv4</strong> setting in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>

</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>Pseudo IPv4</strong>, choose your desired setting.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To change <strong>Pseudo IPv4</strong> with the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/zones/subresources/settings/methods/edit/"><code>PATCH</code></a> request with <code>pseudo_ipv4</code> as the setting name in the URI path, and the <code>value</code> parameter set to your desired value: <code>&quot;off&quot;</code>, <code>&quot;add_header&quot;</code>, or <code>&quot;overwrite_header&quot;</code>.</p>
