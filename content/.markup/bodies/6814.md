Cloudflare recommends that you leave this option selected. If you unselect **Add routes for your Cloudflare WAN address space to your cloud network**, you will need to manually create all the required configurations to allow Cloudflare WAN to connect to your cloud, such as routing tables, transit gateways, and VPNs. Refer to the [Cloudflare WAN How to](/cloudflare-wan/configuration/how-to/) section, or consult the documentation for your cloud provider for more information.
