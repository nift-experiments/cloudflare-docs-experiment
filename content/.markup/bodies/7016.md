The main reason to use the bootstrapper is if every network your Cloudflare One Appliance device is plugged into is either static, behind a VLAN, or both. If you find yourself here and configuring a network with DHCP and no VLAN, you are probably not in the right place. See the section on configuring your Cloudflare One Appliance <a href="#set-up-cloudflare-dashboard">via the dashboard</a>.
