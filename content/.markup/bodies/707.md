CNI supports BGP peering with the Cloudflare Virtual Network routing table. BGP over CNI is in closed beta and is not currently available to new customers. If you are interested in BGP peering over CNI, contact your account team.

For detailed information about BGP architecture, Edge Resiliency Mode, and route propagation behavior, refer to the BGP reference documentation in [Magic Transit](/magic-transit/reference/traffic-steering/#bgp-information) or [Cloudflare WAN](/cloudflare-wan/reference/traffic-steering/#bgp-information) depending on your use case.
