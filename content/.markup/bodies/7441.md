<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Workers &amp; Pages</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select your Pages project.</li>
<li>Follow these steps to <a href="/pages/configuration/custom-domains/">create a Custom Domain</a>.</li>
<li>Go to the <strong>DNS</strong> of the zone you configured the Custom Domain for.</li>
<li>From the <strong>Region</strong> dropdown, select the region you would like to use on your domain.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section>

<li>Run the <a href="/data-localization/regional-services/regional-hostnames/#configure-regional-services-via-api">API POST</a> command on the Pages Custom Domain to create a <code>regional_hostnames</code> with a specific Region.</li>
</ol>
