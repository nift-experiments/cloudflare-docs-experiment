
1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the **L3/4 DDoS protection** page.

<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
2. Go to **Advanced Protection** > **Advanced DNS Protection**.
3. Select **Create Advanced DNS Protection rule**. 
4. In **Mode**, select a [mode](/ddos-protection/advanced-ddos-systems/concepts/#mode) for the rule.
5. Under **Set scope**, select a [scope](/ddos-protection/advanced-ddos-systems/concepts/#scope) to determine the range of packets that will be affected by the rule. 
6. Under **Sensitivity**, define the [burst sensitivity](/ddos-protection/advanced-ddos-systems/concepts/#burst-sensitivity), [rate sensitivity](/ddos-protection/advanced-ddos-systems/concepts/#rate-sensitivity), and [profile sensitivity](/ddos-protection/advanced-ddos-systems/concepts/#profile-sensitivity) to determine when to initiate mitigation. 9. Select **Deploy**.
