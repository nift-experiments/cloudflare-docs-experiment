
- **Type**: Defines the purpose of a record. Different types of record require
different information in their corresponding `Content` field. - **Name**:
Identifies the resource that the record resolves to. Depending on the purpose
of the record, the value you want to add to this field will also change. -
**Content**: Contains the core value of a record, depending on the record
type. - **TTL**: Controls how long each record is cached by DNS resolvers.
