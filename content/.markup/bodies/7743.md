
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>DNS Settings</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select <strong>Enable DNSSEC</strong> and <strong>Confirm</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
<p>For the purpose of this tutorial, you will update your registrar with the DS record later, in <a href="/dns/dnssec/multi-signer-dnssec/setup/#3-set-up-registrar">Step 3</a>.</p>
</aside>
<ol start="3">
<li>Also enable <strong>Multi-signer DNSSEC</strong> and <strong>Multi-provider DNS</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/dns/records"><strong>DNS Records</strong></a> page and create the following records at your zone apex (meaning you should use <code>@</code> in the record <strong>Name</strong> field):</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li>A <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/reference/dns-record-types/#ds-and-dnskey">DNSKEY record</a> with the zone signing key(s) (ZSKs) of your external provider(s).</li>
<li>An <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/reference/dns-record-types/#ns">NS record</a> with your external provider nameservers.</li>
</ul>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<ol>
<li>Use the <a href="/api/resources/dns/subresources/dnssec/methods/edit/">Edit DNSSEC Status endpoint</a> to enable DNSSEC and activate multi-signer DNSSEC for your zone. Set <code>status</code> to <code>active</code> and <code>dnssec_multi_signer</code> to <code>true</code>, as in the following example.</li>
</ol>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request PATCH \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/{zone_id}/dnssec \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;status&quot;: &quot;active&quot;,
&quot;dnssec_multi_signer&quot;: true
}&#x27;</code></pre>
<ol start="2">
<li>Add the ZSK(s) of your external provider(s) to Cloudflare by creating a DNSKEY record on your zone.</li>
</ol>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/{zone_id}/dns_records \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;type&quot;: &quot;DNSKEY&quot;,
&quot;name&quot;: &quot;&lt;ZONE_NAME&gt;&quot;,
&quot;data&quot;: {
  &quot;flags&quot;: 256,
  &quot;protocol&quot;: 3,
  &quot;algorithm&quot;: 13,
  &quot;public_key&quot;: &quot;&lt;PUBLIC_KEY&gt;&quot;
},
&quot;ttl&quot;: 3600
}&#x27;</code></pre>
<ol start="3">
<li>Add your external provider(s) nameservers as NS records on your zone apex.</li>
</ol>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/{zone_id}/dns_records \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;type&quot;: &quot;NS&quot;,
&quot;name&quot;: &quot;&lt;ZONE_NAME&gt;&quot;,
&quot;content&quot;: &quot;&lt;NS_DOMAIN&gt;&quot;,
&quot;ttl&quot;: 86400
}&#x27;</code></pre>
<ol start="4">
<li>Enable the usage of the nameservers you added in the previous step by using the API request below.</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside caution">
<p>This step is required. Without turning on this setting, Cloudflare will ignore any <code>NS</code> records created on the zone apex. This means that responses to DNS queries made to the zone apex and requesting <code>NS</code> records will only contain Cloudflare nameservers.</p>
</aside>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request PATCH \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/{zone_id}/dns_settings \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;multi_provider&quot;: true
}&#x27;</code></pre>
