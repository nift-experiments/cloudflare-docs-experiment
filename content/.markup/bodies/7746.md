
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>DNS Settings</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>DNSSEC with Secondary DNS</strong> select <strong>Live signing</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
<p>For the purpose of this tutorial, you will update your registrar with the DS record later, in <a href="/dns/dnssec/multi-signer-dnssec/setup/#3-set-up-registrar">Step 3</a>.</p>
</aside>
<ol start="3">
<li>Also enable <strong>Multi-signer DNSSEC</strong>.</li>
<li>Add the zone signing key(s) (ZSKs) of your external provider(s) to a DNSKEY record at your primary DNS provider. This record should be transferred successfully to Cloudflare.</li>
<li>Add your external provider(s) nameservers as NS records on your zone apex at your primary DNS provider. These records should be transferred successfully to Cloudflare.</li>
</ol>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<ol>
<li>Use the <a href="/api/resources/dns/subresources/dnssec/methods/edit/">Edit DNSSEC Status endpoint</a> to enable DNSSEC and activate multi-signer DNSSEC for your zone. Set <code>status</code> to <code>active</code> and <code>dnssec_multi_signer</code> to <code>true</code>, as in the following example.</li>
</ol>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request PATCH \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/{zone_id}/dnssec \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;status&quot;: &quot;active&quot;,
&quot;dnssec_multi_signer&quot;: true
}&#x27;</code></pre>
<ol start="2">
<li>
<p>Add the ZSK(s) of your external provider(s) to a DNSKEY record at your primary DNS provider. This record should be transferred successfully to Cloudflare.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Add your external provider(s) nameservers as NS records on your zone apex at your primary DNS provider. These records should be transferred successfully to Cloudflare.</p>
</li>
</ol>
