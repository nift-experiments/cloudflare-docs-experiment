
1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the **DNS Records** page.

<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>

2. Select **Add record**.
3. Select `A`, `AAAA`, or `CNAME` as the record **Type**, according to your needs:
- To point to an IPv4 address, select `A`, use your subdomain (<code>blog</code>) for the record **Name**, and insert the IPv4 address in the respective field.
- To point to an IPv6 address, select `AAAA`, use your subdomain (<code>blog</code>) for the record **Name**, and insert the IPv6 address in the respective field.
- To point to a [fully qualified domain name (FQDN)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fully_qualified_domain_name) (such as `your-site.host.example.com`), select `CNAME`, use your subdomain (<code>blog</code>) for the record **Name**, and insert the fully qualified domain name in the **Target** field.
4. Specify the <div class="nb-interactive-component" data-cf-component="GlossaryTooltip">

<p><strong>Proxy status</strong></p>

</div> and <div class="nb-interactive-component" data-cf-component="GlossaryTooltip">

<p><strong>TTL</strong></p>

</div> according to your needs.
5. Select **Save** to confirm.
