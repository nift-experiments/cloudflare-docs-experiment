<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To delete DNS records in the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>DNS Records</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>On a specific record, select <strong>Edit</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Delete</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Delete</strong> again to confirm.</li>
</ol>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To delete records with the API, use a <a href="/api/resources/dns/subresources/records/methods/delete/">DELETE request</a>.</p>

</section>
