<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>DNS Records</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>On <strong>Custom nameservers</strong>, select <strong>Configure</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Create custom nameservers just for <code>your-domain.com</code></strong> and enter the subdomains used for the ZCNS names (for example, <code>ns1</code>, <code>ns2</code>, <code>ns3</code>).</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong> to confirm.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>Use the <a href="/api/resources/zones/methods/edit/">Edit zone endpoint</a> and specify the custom nameservers in the payload:</p>
<pre><code class="language-txt">&quot;vanity_name_servers&quot;: [&quot;ns1.example.com&quot;,&quot;ns2.example.com&quot;]
</code></pre>
