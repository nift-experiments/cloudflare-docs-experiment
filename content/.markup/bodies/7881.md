<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>DNS Settings</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>Custom nameservers</strong>, select <strong>Configure</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Use your account custom nameservers</strong> and choose a nameserver set from the list.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong> to confirm.</li>
</ol>

</section>
