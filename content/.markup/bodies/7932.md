<aside class="nb-aside caution">
<h3 class="nb-aside-title" id="body-7932-make-sure-you-have-the-correct-plan">Make sure you have the correct plan</h3>
<p>Make sure your zone is on the Business or Enterprise plan. If you have Free or Pro, the options mentioned below will not be displayed.</p>
</aside>
<div class="nb-steps">
<ol>
<li>On the <strong>Overview</strong> page, select <strong>Convert to CNAME DNS Setup</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Convert</strong> to confirm.</li>
<li>Save the information from the <strong>Verification TXT Record</strong>. If you lose the information, you can also access it on the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/dns/records"><strong>DNS Records</strong></a> page, under <strong>Verification TXT Record</strong>.</li>
<li>Make sure that you have all the DNS records (A, AAAA, or CNAME) for subdomains that you want to proxy through Cloudflare.</li>
</ol>
</div>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>If you are adding a zone for the first time via API you can add it directly with a <code>type</code> of <code>partial</code>, without converting it.</p>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request POST \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;name&quot;: &quot;example.com&quot;,
&quot;account&quot;: {
  &quot;id&quot;: &quot;YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID&quot;
},
&quot;type&quot;: &quot;partial&quot;
}&#x27;</code></pre>
