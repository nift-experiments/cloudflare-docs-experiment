


Cloudflare can [automatically scan for your records](/dns/zone-setups/reference/dns-quick-scan/) and add them to the <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="zone">DNS zone</span> for you, or you can add records manually. These records show up under your domain on the [**DNS Records**](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/dns/records) page of the dashboard.


<aside class="nb-aside note">


<p>If you add a zone via the <a href="/api/resources/zones/methods/create/">API</a>, you can manually invoke the quick scan with the <a href="/api/resources/dns/subresources/records/methods/scan_trigger/">Trigger DNS Records Scan endpoint</a>.</p>


</aside>
