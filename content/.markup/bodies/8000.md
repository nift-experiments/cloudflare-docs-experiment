

To export records using the API, send a [GET request](/api/resources/dns/subresources/records/methods/export/).



<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request GET \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/{zone_id}/dns_records/export \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot;</code></pre>
