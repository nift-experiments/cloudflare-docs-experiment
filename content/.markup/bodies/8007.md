
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<ol>
<li>On the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the zone's <strong>Overview</strong> page.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Convert to CNAME DNS Setup</strong> and then <strong>Convert</strong> to confirm.</li>
<li>Save the information from the <strong>Verification TXT Record</strong> and add the record at your new authoritative DNS provider. If you lose the information, you can also access it on the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/dns/records"><strong>DNS Records</strong></a> page, under <strong>Verification TXT Record</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<ol>
<li>Use the <a href="/api/resources/zones/methods/edit/">Edit Zone endpoint</a> with <code>type</code> set to <code>partial</code> to convert the zone type.</li>
<li>Take note of the value returned under <code>verification_key</code> in the API response and add the corresponding TXT record at your new authoritative DNS provider.</li>
</ol>
