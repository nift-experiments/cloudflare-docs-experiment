</li>
<li>
<p>In your child zone, <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/create-dns-records/">re-create all DNS records</a> that relate to your subdomain. This includes all DNS records deeper than the delegated subdomain, meaning that if you are delegating <code>www.example.com</code>, you should also move over records for <code>api.www.example.com</code>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
<p>If your child zone is on a primary setup (full), consider <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/import-and-export/#export-records">exporting</a> records from the parent zone, deleting all unnecessary records, and then <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/import-and-export/#import-records">importing</a> the records into your new zone.</p>
</aside>

<li>

</li>
<li>

</li>
<li>
<p>Get the Cloudflare nameservers for the subdomain and add them as <code>NS</code> records at your external DNS provider.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Within a short period of time, the child zone should be active.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/dns/records"><strong>DNS Records</strong></a> page of the parent zone, <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/create-dns-records/#delete-dns-records">delete</a> any <code>A</code>, <code>AAAA</code>, or <code>CNAME</code> records referencing the subdomain or any of its deeper subdomains.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<ol>
<li>
<p>Add the subdomain to a Cloudflare account as a new zone. It can be the same account where the parent zone exists or a different one.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select either Business or Enterprise as your zone plan and complete the onboarding flow according to your needs.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone">Overview page</a>, select <strong>Convert to CNAME DNS Setup</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>In your child zone, <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/create-dns-records/">re-create all DNS records</a> that relate to your subdomain. This includes all DNS records deeper than the subdomain you used to create the zone - if you are creating a zone for <code>www.example.com</code>, you should also move over records for <code>api.www.example.com</code>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
<p>Cloudflare recommends <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/import-and-export/#export-records">exporting</a> records from the parent zone, deleting all unnecessary records, and then <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/import-and-export/#import-records">importing</a> the records into your new zone.</p>
</aside>
<ol start="5">
<li>
<p>Make sure that you migrate over any settings (<a href="/waf/custom-rules/">WAF custom rules</a>, <a href="/rules/">Rules</a>, <a href="/workers/">Workers</a>, and more) that might be needed for the child zone.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>In the child zone, <a href="/ssl/edge-certificates/advanced-certificate-manager/">order an advanced SSL certificate</a> that covers the child subdomain and any deeper subdomains.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/dns/records"><strong>DNS Records</strong></a> page, get the <strong>Verification TXT Record</strong> and add it at your authoritative DNS provider.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p>If your authoritative DNS provider automatically appends DNS record <code>name</code> fields with your domain, make sure to only insert <code>cloudflare-verify</code> as the record name. Otherwise, it may result in an incorrect record name, such as <code>cloudflare-verify.sub.example.com.sub.example.com</code>.</p>
<p>After creating the record, you can use this <a href="https://digwebinterface.com/?type=TXT&amp;ns=auth&amp;nameservers=">Dig Web Interface link</a> to search (<code>dig</code>) for <code>cloudflare-verify.&lt;YOUR DOMAIN&gt;</code> and validate if it is working.</p>
</div></details>
<p>That record must remain in place for as long as your subdomain is active on the CNAME setup (partial) on Cloudflare.</p>
<ol start="8">
<li>Within a short period of time, the child zone should be active.</li>
<li>On the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/dns/records"><strong>DNS Records</strong></a> page of the parent zone, <a href="/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/create-dns-records/#delete-dns-records">delete</a> any previous <code>A</code>, <code>AAAA</code>, or <code>CNAME</code> records referencing the subdomain or any of its deeper subdomains.</li>
<li>At your authoritative DNS provider, confirm you have <code>CNAME</code> records pointing to <code>{your-hostname}.cdn.cloudflare.net</code> for the subdomain you have added and any deeper subdomain records you want to proxy through Cloudflare.</li>
</ol>
