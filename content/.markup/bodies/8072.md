<p>To link a primary zone to a peer using the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>DNS Settings</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>DNS Zone Transfers</strong>, select <strong>Manage linked peers</strong>.</li>
<li>Select a peer.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>
