</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>Send the following <code>PATCH</code> request replacing the placeholders with your zone ID and authentication information:</p>
<pre class="nb-api-request"><code class="language-bash">curl --request PATCH \
--url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/{zone_id}/dns_settings \
--header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
--data &#x27;{
&quot;multi_provider&quot;: true
}&#x27;</code></pre>
