
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="MX records">

<p><strong>Purpose</strong>: Route incoming emails to Cloudflare's mail servers.</p>
<pre><code class="language-txt">MX yourdomain.com route1.mx.cloudflare.net
MX yourdomain.com route2.mx.cloudflare.net
MX yourdomain.com route3.mx.cloudflare.net
</code></pre>
<p><strong>Configuration:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Type</strong>: MX</li>
<li><strong>Name</strong>: <code>@</code> (root domain)</li>
<li><strong>Mail server</strong>: Cloudflare routing MX servers</li>
<li><strong>Priority</strong>: Assigned automatically by Cloudflare</li>
</ul>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="SPF record">

<p><strong>Purpose</strong>: Authorizes Cloudflare to forward emails on behalf of your domain.</p>
<pre><code class="language-txt">TXT yourdomain.com &quot;v=spf1 include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net ~all&quot;
</code></pre>
<p><strong>Configuration:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Type</strong>: TXT</li>
<li><strong>Name</strong>: <code>@</code> (root domain)</li>
<li><strong>Value</strong>: <code>v=spf1 include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net ~all</code></li>
<li><strong>TTL</strong>: Auto</li>
</ul>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
<h3 class="nb-aside-title" id="body-8636-existing-spf-records">Existing SPF records</h3>
<p>If you have existing SPF records, merge them: <code>v=spf1 include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net include:_spf.google.com ~all</code>.</p>
<p>SPF records are limited to 10 DNS lookups total. If merging pushes you over the limit, consider flattening the record or removing non-essential <code>include:</code> entries.</p>
</aside>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="DKIM record">

<p><strong>Purpose</strong>: Provides cryptographic authentication for forwarded emails.</p>
<pre><code class="language-txt">TXT cf2024-1._domainkey.yourdomain.com &quot;v=DKIM1; h=sha256; k=rsa; p=MIIBIjANBgkqhkiG9w0BAQEFAAOCAQ8AMIIBCgKCAQEA...&quot;
</code></pre>
<p><strong>Configuration:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Type</strong>: TXT</li>
<li><strong>Name</strong>: <code>cf2024-1._domainkey</code> (selector provided by Cloudflare)</li>
<li><strong>Value</strong>: DKIM public key (provided by Cloudflare)</li>
<li><strong>TTL</strong>: Auto</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Separate from sending DKIM</strong> - Email Routing uses its own DKIM selector (<code>cf2024-1._domainkey</code>) and keys, distinct from the sending DKIM selector (<code>cf-bounce._domainkey</code>).</p>

</section>
