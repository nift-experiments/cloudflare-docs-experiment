<p><strong>Issue</strong>: Records show as &quot;Not Found&quot; immediately after adding.</p>
<p><strong>Solution</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Wait 5-15 minutes for DNS propagation</li>
<li>Check propagation status: <code>dig TXT yourdomain.com</code></li>
<li>Cloudflare domains propagate faster than external domains</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Check propagation globally:</strong></p>
<pre><code class="language-bash"># Check sending SPF record
dig TXT cf-bounce.yourdomain.com | grep spf

# Check routing SPF record
dig TXT yourdomain.com | grep spf

# Check sending DKIM record
dig TXT cf-bounce._domainkey.yourdomain.com

# Check routing DKIM record
dig TXT cf2024-1._domainkey.yourdomain.com

# Check routing MX records
dig MX yourdomain.com

# Check sending MX records (bounce handling)
dig MX cf-bounce.yourdomain.com
</code></pre>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Record conflicts">

<p><strong>Issue</strong>: Existing DNS records conflict with Email Service.</p>
<p><strong>SPF conflicts:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Merge existing SPF records</li>
<li>Remove duplicate <code>v=spf1</code> entries</li>
<li>Ensure only one SPF record exists</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>MX conflicts:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Email Routing requires Cloudflare MX records</li>
<li>Remove or update existing MX records</li>
<li>Cannot use Email Routing with external mail servers</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>DKIM conflicts:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Use different selectors for different services</li>
<li><code>cf-bounce._domainkey</code> for Email Sending</li>
<li><code>cf2024-1._domainkey</code> for Email Routing</li>
<li><code>google._domainkey</code> for Google Workspace</li>
</ul>

</section>
