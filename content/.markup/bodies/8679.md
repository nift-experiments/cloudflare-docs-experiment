<p>Add a <code>Cc</code> header for CC recipients. BCC recipients are added as <code>--mail-rcpt</code> flags but omitted from the headers.</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">cat &gt; mail.txt &lt;&lt;EOF
From: orders@yourdomain.com
To: customer@example.com
Cc: manager@example.com
Reply-To: support@yourdomain.com
Subject: Order Confirmation #12345

Your order is confirmed
EOF

curl --ssl-reqd \
  --url &quot;smtps://smtp.mx.cloudflare.net:465&quot; \
  --user &quot;api_token:&lt;API_TOKEN&gt;&quot; \
  --mail-from &quot;orders@yourdomain.com&quot; \
  --mail-rcpt &quot;customer@example.com&quot; \
  --mail-rcpt &quot;manager@example.com&quot; \
  --mail-rcpt &quot;archive@example.com&quot; \
  --upload-file mail.txt
</code></pre>
