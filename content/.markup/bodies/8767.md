

[Smart Shield Advanced](/smart-shield/get-started/#packages-and-availability) provides dedicated egress IPs (from Cloudflare to your origin) for your layer 7 [WAF](/waf/) and <div class="nb-interactive-component" data-cf-component="GlossaryTooltip">

<p>CDN</p>

</div>  services, as well as [Spectrum](/spectrum/). The egress IPs are reserved exclusively for your account so that you can increase your origin security by only allowing a small list of IP addresses through your <div class="nb-interactive-component" data-cf-component="GlossaryTooltip">

<p>layer 3</p>

</div> firewall.

* **Security**: Very secure.
* **Availability**: Enterprise-only.
* **Challenges**: Requires network-level firewall policies.
