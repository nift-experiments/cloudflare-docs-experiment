<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To restart a failed or timed out verification, send a <code>PATCH</code> request with the existing <code>client_uri</code> unchanged.</p>
<pre><code class="language-bash">curl -X PATCH &quot;https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/$ACCOUNT_ID/oauth_clients/$CLIENT_ID&quot; \
	&#45;H &quot;Content-Type: application/json&quot; \
	&#45;H &quot;Authorization: Bearer $API_TOKEN&quot; \
	&#45;d &#x27;{ &quot;client_uri&quot;: &quot;https://example.com&quot; }&#x27;
</code></pre>
