<div class="nb-steps">
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>Manage Account</strong> &gt; <strong>OAuth clients</strong>.</li>
<li>Open the action menu for your client.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Rotate client secret</strong>.</li>
<li>Save the new secret in a secure location.</li>
<li>After your client uses the new secret, delete the old secret.</li>
</ol>
</div>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To check whether a client is in the middle of a secret rotation, look for <code>has_rotated_secret</code> in the <code>GET</code> response. If the value is <code>true</code>, delete the old secret before you create another secret.</p>
<h4 id="body-8846-create-a-new-secret">Create a new secret</h4>
<pre><code class="language-bash">curl -X POST &quot;https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/$ACCOUNT_ID/oauth_clients/$CLIENT_ID/rotate_secret&quot; \
	&#45;H &quot;Content-Type: application/json&quot; \
	&#45;H &quot;Authorization: Bearer $API_TOKEN&quot;
</code></pre>
<h4 id="body-8846-delete-the-old-secret">Delete the old secret</h4>
<pre><code class="language-bash">curl -X DELETE &quot;https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/$ACCOUNT_ID/oauth_clients/$CLIENT_ID/rotate_secret&quot; \
	&#45;H &quot;Content-Type: application/json&quot; \
	&#45;H &quot;Authorization: Bearer $API_TOKEN&quot;
</code></pre>
