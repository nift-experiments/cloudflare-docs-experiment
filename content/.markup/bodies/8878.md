<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To revoke a member's access to your account:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Members</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Locate an account member and expand their record.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Revoke</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Yes, revoke access</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To revoke a member's access to your account using the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/accounts/subresources/members/methods/delete/"><code>DELETE</code> request</a>.</p>

</section>
