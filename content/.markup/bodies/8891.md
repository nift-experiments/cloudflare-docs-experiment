<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>Enable the connector by running the following — again, replacing the <code>{account_id}</code> value with your account ID, and additionally replacing the <code>{sso_connector_id}</code> with the value you obtained from the <code>id</code> field in the response to the previous call.</p>
<pre><code class="language-bash">curl &quot;https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/sso_connectors/{sso_connector_id}&quot; \
  --request PATCH \
  --header &quot;Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN&quot; \
  --json '{&quot;enabled&quot;: true}'
</code></pre>
