Hyperdrive does not have a limit on the number of concurrent _client_ connections made from your Workers to Hyperdrive.

Hyperdrive does have a limit of _origin_ connections that can be made from Hyperdrive to your database. These are shared across Workers, with each Worker using one of these connections over the course of a database transaction. Refer to [transaction pooling mode](/hyperdrive/concepts/connection-pooling/#pooling-mode) for more information.
