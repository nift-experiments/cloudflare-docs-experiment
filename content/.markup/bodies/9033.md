

Use the [Hyperdrive REST API](/api/resources/hyperdrive/subresources/configs/methods/update/) to update your configuration:

```sh
curl --request PATCH \
  --url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/<ACCOUNT_ID>/hyperdrive/configs/<HYPERDRIVE_ID> \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'Authorization: Bearer <API_TOKEN>' \
  --data '{
    "origin_connection_limit": <MAX_CONNECTIONS>
  }'
```
