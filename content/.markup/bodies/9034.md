
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To configure connection pool size via the dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com">Cloudflare dashboard</a> and select your account.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Storage &amp; databases</strong> &gt; <strong>Hyperdrive</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="3">
<li>Select your Hyperdrive configuration.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>In the <strong>Origin connection limit</strong> section, adjust the <strong>Maximum connections</strong> value.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Wrangler">

<p>Use the <a href="/hyperdrive/reference/wrangler-commands/#hyperdrive-update"><code>wrangler hyperdrive update</code></a> command with the <code>--origin-connection-limit</code> flag:</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">npx wrangler hyperdrive update &lt;HYPERDRIVE_ID&gt; --origin-connection-limit=&lt;MAX_CONNECTIONS&gt;
</code></pre>

</section>

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>Use the <a href="/api/resources/hyperdrive/subresources/configs/methods/update/">Hyperdrive REST API</a> to update your configuration:</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">curl --request PATCH \
  --url https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/&lt;ACCOUNT_ID&gt;/hyperdrive/configs/&lt;HYPERDRIVE_ID&gt; \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'Authorization: Bearer &lt;API_TOKEN&gt;' \
  --data '{
    &quot;origin_connection_limit&quot;: &lt;MAX_CONNECTIONS&gt;
  }'
</code></pre>

</section>
