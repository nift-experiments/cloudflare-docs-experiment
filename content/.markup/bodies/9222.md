

<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="&quot;Dashboard&quot;">

<div class="nb-steps">
<ol>
<li>From the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/hyperdrive">Cloudflare Hyperdrive dashboard</a>, select your newly created Hyperdrive configuration.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Origin connection limit</strong>, select <strong>Edit Settings</strong>, and set your maximum connections to a number that is lower than your Prisma connection limit.</li>
</ol>
</div>

</section>
<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="&quot;Wrangler CLI&quot;">

<div class="nb-steps">
<ol>
<li>Edit your existing Hyperdrive configuration with the <code>--origin-connection-limit</code> parameter:</li>
</ol>
<pre><code class="language-bash">npx wrangler hyperdrive update &lt;HYPERDRIVE_ID&gt; --origin-connection-limit=10
</code></pre>
<p>Replace <code>&lt;HYPERDRIVE_ID&gt;</code> with your Hyperdrive configuration ID and set the connection limit to a number that is less than your Prisma connection limit.
2. Verify the configuration change:</p>
<pre><code class="language-bash">npx wrangler hyperdrive get &lt;HYPERDRIVE_ID&gt;
</code></pre>
</div>

</section>
