<section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="Dashboard">

<p>To add a member to your account:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Members</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="3">
<li>
<p>Select <strong>Invite</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Fill out the following information:</p>
</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Invite members</strong>: Enter one or more email addresses (if multiple, separate addresses with commas).</li>
<li><strong>Scope</strong>: Use a variety of fields to adjust the <a href="/fundamentals/manage-members/roles/">scope</a> of your roles.</li>
<li><strong>Roles</strong>: Choose one or more <a href="/fundamentals/manage-members/roles/">roles</a> to assign your members.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="5">
<li>
<p>Select <strong>Continue to summary</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Review the information, then select <strong>Invite</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
<p>If a user already has an account with Cloudflare and you have an Enterprise account, you can also select <strong>Skip email confirmation</strong> to add them to your account without sending an email invitation.</p>
</aside>

</section> <section class="nb-tab-panel" role="tabpanel" data-nb-tabs-content data-nb-tab-label="API">

<p>To add a member using the API, send a <a href="/api/resources/accounts/subresources/members/methods/create/"><code>POST</code> request</a>.</p>

</section>
