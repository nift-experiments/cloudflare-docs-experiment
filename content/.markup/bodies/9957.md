


* The CA certificate can be from a publicly trusted CA or self-signed.
* In the certificate `Basic Constraints`, the attribute `CA` must be set to `TRUE`.
* The certificate must use one of the signature algorithms listed below:

<details class="nb-details"><summary>Allowed signature algorithms</summary><div class="nb-details-body">

<p><code>x509.SHA1WithRSA</code></p>
<p><code>x509.SHA256WithRSA</code></p>
<p><code>x509.SHA384WithRSA</code></p>
<p><code>x509.SHA512WithRSA</code></p>
<p><code>x509.ECDSAWithSHA1</code></p>
<p><code>x509.ECDSAWithSHA256</code></p>
<p><code>x509.ECDSAWithSHA384</code></p>
<p><code>x509.ECDSAWithSHA512</code></p>

</div></details>
