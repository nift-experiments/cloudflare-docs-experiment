
It is not recommended to employ a default-deny model while testing. Instead, build your explicit application policies and [monitor your logs](/cloudflare-one/insights/logs/dashboard-logs/gateway-logs/) to determine if your policies are working as expected. If you do not see the policies triggering in your logs, you may need to tune your policies and review what kind of information (identity groups, device posture values, etc.) is being sent with your traffic.
