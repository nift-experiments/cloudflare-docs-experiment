<p>After <a href="/1.1.1.1/setup/">setting up 1.1.1.1</a>, you can verify that your DNS queries are going through Cloudflare's resolver.</p>
<ol>
<li>Open a web browser on a device that you configured to use 1.1.1.1, or on a device connected to a router you configured.</li>
<li>Go to <a href="https://one.one.one.one/help">https://1.1.1.1/help</a>.</li>
</ol>
<p>The page runs a series of tests and shows whether your connection to 1.1.1.1 is working. It also displays which Cloudflare data center is serving your requests.</p>
