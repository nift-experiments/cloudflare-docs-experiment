<p>Total Threats Stopped measures the number of 'suspicious' and 'bad' requests that were aimed at your site. Requests receive these labels as they enter Cloudflare's network:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Legitimate:</strong> Request passed directly to your site.</li>
<li><strong>Suspicious:</strong> Request has been challenged with a <a href="/cloudflare-challenges/">Cloudflare challenge</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Bad:</strong> Request has been blocked because our Browser Integrity Check, or because of user configured settings like WAF rules or IP Access rules.</li>
</ul>
<p>In addition to threat analytics you can also monitor search engine crawlers going to your websites. For most websites, threats and crawlers make up 20% to 50% of traffic.</p>
