@markup("md", "content/.markup/bodies/1512.md")

<p>Argo Smart Routing detects real-time network issues and routes your web traffic across the most efficient network path, avoiding congestion.</p>
<p>This results in faster loading times, increased reliability, and reduced costs. These benefits are most apparent for users farthest from your origin server.</p>
