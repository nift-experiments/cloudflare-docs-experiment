<p>Before you begin using APO, we recommend testing your current site speed. Your site speed results give your website a letter grade and performance rating.</p>
<ol>
<li>Visit <a href="https://gtmetrix.com/">GTmetrix</a>.</li>
<li>In the text field, enter your website's URL.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Test your site</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>After the test runs, you can view your site's grade, performance rating, and other performance metrics.</p>
