<p>Before you change your nameservers, confirm your DNS records are displaying correctly.</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the DNS <strong>Records</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>To add a record, select <strong>Add record</strong>.</li>
<li>To edit an existing record, select <strong>Edit</strong> for the appropriate record.</li>
<li>After making your changes, select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>After you confirm your DNS records, <a href="/automatic-platform-optimization/get-started/change-nameservers/">change your nameservers</a>.</p>
