<h2 id="add-a-primary-payment-method">Add a primary payment method</h2>
<p>A primary payment method is required to purchase Cloudflare products and services. Cloudflare does not store or have access to your full card number, PIN, or PayPal password.</p>

<h2 id="add-an-additional-payment-method">Add an additional payment method</h2>
<p>Optionally, add an additional payment method. Cloudflare automatically retries the charge on the additional method if the primary method fails. Refer to <a href="/billing/payment-methods/additional-payment-method-auto-retry/">Additional payment method auto-retry</a> for details.</p>
