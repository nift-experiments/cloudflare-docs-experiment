<p>You will receive an invoice in the <strong>Billing</strong> section of your Cloudflare account when you:</p>
<ul>
<li>Change your Cloudflare plan type.</li>
<li>Upgrade or downgrade to or from a paid plan.</li>
<li>Add a new domain to a Cloudflare account.</li>
<li>Turn on or renew a subscription or add-on service.</li>
</ul>
<p>For any historical invoices not included in the <strong>Billing</strong> section, <a href="/support/contacting-cloudflare-support/">contact Cloudflare support</a>.</p>

<h2 id="billing-cycles">Billing cycles</h2>
<p>Monthly and annual billing subscriptions run on different billing cycles.</p>
<p>The first monthly purchase on a Cloudflare account sets the billing date for the following monthly subscriptions. The same behavior occurs for annual subscriptions.</p>
<p>You can have two different billing cycles on your account, one for a monthly subscription and another for an annual subscription.</p>
