<p>Review the definitions for terms used across Cloudflare's Bots documentation.</p>
<table id="glossary-table"><thead><tr><th>Term</th><th>Definition</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>bot</td><td><p>A software application programmed to do tasks that can be used for good (chatbots, search engine crawlers) or for evil (inventory hoarding, credential stuffing).</p></td></tr><tr><td>bot score</td><td><p>A score from 1 to 99 that indicates how likely that request came from a bot, in which 1 to 29 is likely automated and 30 to 99 is likely human.</p></td></tr><tr><td>bot tags</td><td><p>Additional information about a bot request, such as why Cloudflare has given it a bot score and whether the request came from a verified bot or a category of verified bots.</p></td></tr><tr><td>Challenge solve rate (CSR)</td><td><p>The percentage of issued challenges that were solved.</p></td></tr><tr><td>detection ID</td><td><p>Static rules that are used to detect predictable bot behavior with no overlap with human traffic.</p></td></tr><tr><td>direct</td><td><p>A label applied to a verified bot or agent operated by a single, narrow operator, usually on the operator's own infrastructure. Replaces the standalone &quot;verified bot&quot; classification used before July 1, 2026.</p></td></tr><tr><td>intermediary</td><td><p>A label applied to a verified agent that a wide range of end users can operate, such as a browser-use or agentic service. Replaces the &quot;signed agent&quot; classification used before July 1, 2026.</p></td></tr><tr><td>JA3 fingerprint</td><td><p>JA3 and JA4 fingerprints profile specific SSL/TLS clients across different destination IPs, Ports, and X509 certificates.</p></td></tr><tr><td>signed agent</td><td><p>A deprecated classification (retired July 1, 2026) for end-user-controlled agents that self-identify with Web Bot Auth. These agents are now verified bots labeled as intermediary.</p></td></tr><tr><td>verified bot</td><td><p>A bot or agent that Cloudflare has confirmed is transparent about who it is and what it does: it represents itself honestly and does not abuse the access that honesty earns.</p></td></tr></tbody></table>
