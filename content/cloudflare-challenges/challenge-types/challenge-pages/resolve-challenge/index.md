<p>If a visitor encounters a Challenge, Cloudflare employees cannot remove that Challenge. Only the website owner can configure their Cloudflare settings to stop the Challenge being presented.</p>
<p>When observing a Cloudflare Challenge page, a visitor could:</p>
<ul>
<li>Successfully pass the Challenge to visit the website.</li>
<li>Request the website owner to allow their IP address.</li>
<li>Scan their computer for malicious programs (it may be infected).</li>
<li>Check their antivirus or firewall service to make sure it is not blocking access to the Challenge resources (for example, images).</li>
</ul>
