<p>Cloudflare partners with many <a href="/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/saas-customers/provider-guides/">SaaS providers</a> to extend our performance and security benefits to your website.</p>
<p>If you are a SaaS customer, you can take this process a step further by managing your own zone on Cloudflare. This setup - known as <strong>O2O</strong> - allows you to benefit from your provider's setup but still customize how Cloudflare treats incoming traffic to your zone.</p>
