<p>When you <a href="/notifications/get-started/configure-webhooks/">create a webhook notification</a> for <strong>SSL for SaaS Custom Hostnames</strong>, you may want to automate responses to specific events (certificate issuance, failed validation, etc.).</p>
<p>The following section details the data Cloudflare sends to a webhook destination.</p>
<h2 id="certificate-validation">Certificate validation</h2>
<p>Before a Certificate Authority will issue a certificate for a domain, the requester must prove they have control over that domain. This process is known as <a href="/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/security/certificate-management/issue-and-validate/validate-certificates/">domain control validation (DCV)</a>.</p>
<h3 id="validation-succeeded">Validation succeeded</h3>
<p>Cloudflare sends this alert when certificates move from a status of <code>pending_validation</code> to <code>pending_issuance</code>.</p>
<pre tabindex="0"><code class="language-json">{&#10;  &quot;metadata&quot;: {&#10;    &quot;event&quot;: {&#10;      &quot;id&quot;: &quot;&lt;&lt;WEBHOOK_ID&gt;&quot;,&#10;      &quot;type&quot;: &quot;ssl.custom_hostname_certificate.validation.succeeded&quot;,&#10;      &quot;created_at&quot;: &quot;2022-02-09T00:03:28.385080Z&quot;&#10;    },&#10;    &quot;account&quot;: {&#10;      &quot;id&quot;: &quot;&lt;&lt;ACCOUNT_ID&gt;&quot;&#10;    },&#10;    &quot;zone&quot;: {&#10;      &quot;id&quot;: &quot;&lt;&lt;ZONE_ID&gt;&quot;&#10;    }&#10;  },&#10;  &quot;data&quot;: {&#10;    &quot;id&quot;: &quot;&lt;&lt;CUSTOM_HOSTNAME_ID&gt;&quot;,&#10;    &quot;hostname&quot;: &quot;blog.com&quot;,&#10;    &quot;ssl&quot;: {&#10;      &quot;id&quot;: &quot;&lt;&lt;CERTIFICATE_ID&gt;&quot;,&#10;      &quot;type&quot;: &quot;dv&quot;,&#10;      &quot;method&quot;: &quot;cname&quot;,&#10;      &quot;status&quot;: &quot;pending_issuance&quot;,&#10;      &quot;settings&quot;: {&#10;        &quot;min_tls_version&quot;: &quot;1.2&quot;,&#10;        &quot;http2&quot;: &quot;on&quot;&#10;      }&#10;    },&#10;    &quot;custom_metadata&quot;: {&#10;      &quot;key1&quot;: &quot;value1&quot;,&#10;      &quot;key2&quot;: &quot;value2&quot;&#10;    },&#10;    &quot;custom_origin_server&quot;: &quot;0001.blog.com&quot;&#10;  }&#10;}&#10;</code></pre>

<h2 id="troubleshooting">Troubleshooting</h2>
<p>Occasionally, you may see webhook notifications that do not include a corresponding <code>&lt;&lt;CUSTOM_HOSTNAME_ID&gt;&gt;</code> and <code>hostname</code> values.</p>
<p>This behavior is because each custom hostname can only have one certificate attached to it. Previously attached certificates can still emit webhook events but will not include the associated hostname and ID values.</p>
