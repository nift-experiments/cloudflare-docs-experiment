<p>To enable Cloudflare for SaaS for your account:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Custom Hostnames</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select <strong>Enable</strong>.</li>
<li>The next step depends on the zone's plan:
<ul>
<li><strong>Enterprise</strong>: Eligible accounts can preview this product as a <a href="/billing/understand/preview-services/">non-contract service</a>, which provides full access free of metered usage fees, limits, and certain other restrictions. If you do not have access, contact your account team.</li>
<li><strong>Non-enterprise</strong>: Enter payment information.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
