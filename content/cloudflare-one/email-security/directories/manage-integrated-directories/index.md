<p>To manage an integrated directory:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to <a href="https://one.dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare One</a>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Email security</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Directories</strong>.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Directory name</strong>, select your directory.</li>
<li>You will be redirected to a page where you can manage <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/directories/manage-integrated-directories/manage-groups-directory/">Groups</a> or <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/directories/manage-integrated-directories/manage-users-directory/">Users</a> directories.</li>
</ol>
