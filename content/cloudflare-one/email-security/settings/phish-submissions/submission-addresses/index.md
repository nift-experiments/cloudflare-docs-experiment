<p>To view the destination addresses of user and team submissions:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to <a href="https://one.dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare One</a>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Email security</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Phish submission</strong> &gt; <strong>Submission addresses</strong> &gt; <strong>View</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>The dashboard will display <strong>User submission addresses</strong> and <strong>Team submission addresses</strong>.</p>
