<p>BCC/Journaling deployment is a post-delivery type of deployment. Cloudflare analyzes emails after they reach the user's inbox. Every time you receive an email, your email provider will send a blind copy to Cloudflare for an analysis.</p>
<ul>
<li>Choose <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/setup/post-delivery-deployment/bcc-journaling/bcc-setup/gmail-bcc-setup/gmail-bcc-setup/">BCC</a> if your email provider is Gmail.</li>
<li>Choose <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/setup/post-delivery-deployment/bcc-journaling/journaling-setup/m365-journaling/">Journaling</a> if your email provider is Microsoft 365.</li>
</ul>
