<p>Team submissions are the emails your security team submitted for submission. All team submissions receive a human review by Cloudflare.</p>
<h2 id="view-team-submissions">View team submissions</h2>
<p>To view team submissions:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to <a href="https://one.dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare One</a>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Email security</strong> &gt; <strong>Submissions</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Team submissions</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="filter-team-submissions">Filter team submissions</h2>
<p>Select among the following filters:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Date Range</strong>: You can select a date range from the last 7, last 30, and last 90 days.</li>
<li><strong>Original disposition</strong>: Select among the <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/reference/dispositions-and-attributes/#available-values">available values</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Submitted as</strong>: Select among the <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/reference/dispositions-and-attributes/#available-values">available values</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Final disposition</strong>: Select among the <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/reference/dispositions-and-attributes/#available-values">available values</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Escalation</strong>: Filter by team submissions that have been escalated or not. Select among <code>Yes</code>, <code>No</code>, or <code>All</code>.</li>
</ul>
<p>Once you have selected all the filters, select <strong>Apply filters</strong>.</p>
<p>The dashboard will populate the table with the list of emails your security team submitted for submission, including a <strong>Submission ID</strong>, and the <strong>Email subject</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="view-submission-details">View submission details</h2>
<p>To gain more details on a specific submission:</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to the submission you want to have more details for.</li>
<li>Select the three dots &gt; select among <strong>View more</strong>, <strong>View email message</strong> and <strong>View similar details</strong>.</li>
</ol>
