<p>You can install <code>cloudflared</code> as a system service on Linux and Windows, and as a launch agent on macOS. In most cases, we recommend running <code>cloudflared</code> as a service. Running as a service helps ensure the availability of <code>cloudflared</code> to your origin by allowing the program to start at boot and continue running while your origin is online.</p>
