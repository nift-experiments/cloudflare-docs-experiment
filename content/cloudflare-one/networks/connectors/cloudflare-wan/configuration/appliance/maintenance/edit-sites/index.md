<ol>
<li>Log in to <a href="https://one.dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare One</a> &gt; <strong>Insights</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Network visibility</strong> &gt; <strong>Traffic overview</strong>, and find the site you want to make changes on.</li>
<li>Select the three dots next to it &gt; <strong>Edit</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Basic information</strong>, make changes to the site's name, description, and geographic coordinates.</li>
<li>In <strong>On-ramps</strong>, add new on-ramps to your site. You can also remove existing ones.</li>
</ol>
