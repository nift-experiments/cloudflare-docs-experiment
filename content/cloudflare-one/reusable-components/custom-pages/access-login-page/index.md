<p>You can customize the login page that is displayed to end users when they go to an Access application.</p>
<p>To change the appearance of your login page:</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Reusable components</strong> &gt; <strong>Custom pages</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Find the <strong>Access login page</strong> setting and select <strong>Manage</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Give the login page the look and feel of your organization by adding:</p>
<ul>
<li>Your organization's name</li>
<li>A logo</li>
<li>A custom header and footer</li>
<li>A preferred background color</li>
</ul>
<p>Any changes you make will be reflected in real time in the <strong>Preview</strong> card.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Once you are satisfied with your customization, select <strong>Save</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>The login page is now updated for all of your Access applications.</p>
