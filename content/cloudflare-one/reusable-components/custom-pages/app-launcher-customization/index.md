<p>You can display your own branding, messages, and links to users when they open the <a href="/cloudflare-one/access-controls/access-settings/app-launcher/">Access App Launcher</a>.</p>
<p>To customize the App Launcher appearance:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare dashboard</a>, go to <strong>Zero Trust</strong> &gt; <strong>Reusable components</strong> &gt; <strong>Custom pages</strong>.</li>
<li>Find the <strong>App Launcher customization</strong> setting and select <strong>Manage</strong>.</li>
<li>Give the App Launcher the look and feel of your organization by adding:
<ul>
<li>Your organization's name</li>
<li>A logo</li>
<li>A preferred background color for the header</li>
<li>A preferred background color for the page</li>
<li>A custom footer with links to your organization's help desk or other internal resources.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
@markup("md", "content/.markup/bodies/5902.md")
</aside>
<ol start="4">
<li>
<p>Next, customize the landing page that users will see when they login to the App Launcher. Available properties include:</p>
<ul>
<li>A custom title</li>
<li>A custom subtitle</li>
<li>An image</li>
<li>A preferred color for the <strong>Log in</strong> button</li>
<li>A preferred color for the <strong>Log in</strong> button text</li>
</ul>
<p>All of the properties configured in Step 3 will also apply to the landing page.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Once you are satisfied with your customization, select <strong>Save</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>The App Launcher screens are now updated. To view your changes, select <strong>Preview</strong>.</p>
