<p>Depending on how your organization is structured, you can deploy the Cloudflare One Client (formerly WARP) in one of two ways:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/deployment/manual-deployment/">Manual deployment</a> — Users download and enroll the client themselves. This is the simplest way to get started, best suited for small organizations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/deployment/mdm-deployment/">Managed deployment</a> — Bigger organizations with MDM tools like Intune or JAMF can deploy the Cloudflare One Client to their entire fleet of devices from a single operation.</p>
</li>
</ul>
