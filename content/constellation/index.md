<p>Constellation allows you to run fast, low-latency inference tasks on pre-trained machine learning models natively on Cloudflare Workers. It supports some of the most popular machine learning (ML) and AI runtimes and multiple classes of models.</p>
<p>Cloudflare provides a curated list of verified models, or you can train and upload your own.</p>
<p>Functionality you can deploy to your application with Constellation:</p>
<ul>
<li>Content generation, summarization, or similarity analysis</li>
<li>Question answering</li>
<li>Audio transcription</li>
<li>Image or audio classification</li>
<li>Object detection</li>
<li>Anomaly detection</li>
<li>Sentiment analysis</li>
</ul>
