<p>As part of the Data Localization Suite, the Customer Metadata Boundary (CMB) ensures that Customer Logs stay in the region you select.</p>
<p>Customer Logs are traffic metadata — information generated when visitors access your site, such as request URLs, timestamps, and firewall events — that could identify your end users. These logs are tagged with your <a href="/fundamentals/account/find-account-and-zone-ids/">Account ID</a> and will be stored exclusively in the European Union (<code>eu</code>) or the United States (<code>us</code>), depending on the region you configure. Customer Metadata Boundary supports these two regions only; by default no boundary is applied and logs may be stored in Cloudflare's core data centers globally. CMB uses its own region setting, separate from <a href="/data-localization/region-support/#region-types">Regional Services regions</a>. For example, if you select the <code>eu</code> Customer Metadata Boundary, metadata will <strong>only</strong> be sent to Cloudflare's core data center (the centralized processing facility, as distinct from the globally distributed edge data centers) located in the European Union.</p>
<p>An exception is made if &quot;Allow out-of-region access&quot; is enabled. When enabled, Customer Logs will still be stored in the configured regions but will be accessible to authorized users on your account, regardless of physical location. Refer to <a href="/data-localization/metadata-boundary/out-of-region-access/">Out of region access</a> for more details.</p>

<h2 id="dashboard-analytics-visibility">Dashboard analytics visibility</h2>
<p>When Customer Metadata Boundary is configured, dashboard analytics and Logs views are only accessible to users whose sessions are routed to the configured region's core data center. This is determined by geo-steering, not by your physical location. If your session is routed to a data center outside the configured region, you may see &quot;no data&quot; for analytics and logs.</p>
<p>To allow authorized users to view logs and analytics regardless of where their session is routed, enable <a href="/data-localization/metadata-boundary/out-of-region-access/">Allow out-of-region access</a>.</p>
<h2 id="log-management">Log management</h2>
<p>Additionally, you can configure <a href="/logs/logpush/">Logpush</a> (Cloudflare's log export service) to push Customer Logs to your own storage services, SIEMs (Security Information and Event Management systems), and log management providers.</p>
