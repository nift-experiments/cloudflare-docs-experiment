<p>With <a href="/dns/foundation-dns/">Foundation DNS</a>, you can request that the ZSK/KSK pair that is used for <a href="/dns/dnssec/">DNSSEC</a> is unique to your Cloudflare account. To opt in to this feature, contact your account team.</p>
<p>All zones within your Cloudflare account - regardless of using <a href="/dns/nameservers/#standard-nameservers">standard</a> or <a href="/dns/foundation-dns/advanced-nameservers/">advanced nameservers</a> - will use the dedicated Zone Signing Key (ZSK) and Key Signing Key (KSK) for DNSSEC. These keys are set at the account level.</p>
