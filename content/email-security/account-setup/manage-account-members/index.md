<p>If your account is a <strong>Super Admin</strong>, you have the ability to add, edit, and delete users and - if those users lose their <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="two-factor authentication (2FA)">two-factor authentication (2FA)</span> device - reset their 2FA.</p>
<h2 id="add-user">Add user</h2>
<p>To add a user:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/">Email Security (formerly Area 1) dashboard</a>  .</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> (the gear icon).</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Users and Actions</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add User</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter their information, as well as their <a href="/email-security/account-setup/permissions/"><strong>Permission</strong> level</a>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Send Invitation</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="edit-user">Edit user</h2>
<p>To edit a user's settings:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/">Email security dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> (the gear icon).</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Users and Actions</strong>.</li>
<li>On a specific user, select <strong>...</strong> &gt; <strong>Edit</strong>.</li>
<li>Update any needed information.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Update User</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="delete-user">Delete user</h2>
<p>To delete a user:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/">Email security dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> (the gear icon).</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Users and Actions</strong>.</li>
<li>On a specific user, select <strong>...</strong> &gt; <strong>Delete</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="reset-two-factor-authentication">Reset two-factor authentication</h2>
<p>To reset a user's two-factor authentication (2FA):</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/">Email security dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> (the gear icon).</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Users and Actions</strong>.</li>
<li>On a specific user, select <strong>...</strong> &gt; <strong>Reset 2FA</strong>.</li>
</ol>
