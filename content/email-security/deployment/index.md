<p>When you configure Email Security (formerly Area 1) Email Security to protect your organization, you can choose between two main setup architectures: Inline and API.</p>
<p>With an Inline deployment, Email Security evaluates email messages before they reach a user’s inbox. When you choose an API deployment, email messages only reach Email Security after they have already reached a user’s inbox.</p>
<p>Select a deployment method to get started.</p>
