<p>Alert Webhooks allow you to connect external services to Email security, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>Slack</li>
<li>Email addresses</li>
<li><span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="SIEM"><pre tabindex="0"><code>SIEM&#10;</code></pre></span></li>
<li>Microsoft Teams</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="create-an-alert-webhook">Create an alert webhook</h2>
<p>To create an alert webhook in Email security:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/">Email security dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> (the gear icon).</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Email Configuration</strong> &gt; <strong>Domains &amp; Routing</strong> &gt; <strong>Alert Webhooks</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>New Webhook</strong>.</li>
<li>Select an <strong>App Type</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter the <strong>Target</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Publish Webhook</strong>.</li>
</ol>
