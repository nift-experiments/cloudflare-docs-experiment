<p><span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="phishing">Phishing</span> is the root cause of upwards of 90% of breaches that lead to financial loss and brand damage. Email Security (formerly Area 1) is a cloud-native service that stops phishing attacks across all threat vectors either at the <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/serverless/glossary/what-is-edge-computing/">edge</a> or in the cloud.</p>
<p>With globally distributed sensors and comprehensive attack analytics, Email Security (formerly Area 1) proactively identifies phishing campaigns, attacker infrastructure, and attack delivery mechanisms during the earliest stages of a phishing attack cycle. Using flexible enforcement platforms, the Email Security (formerly Area 1) solution allows customers to take preemptive action against targeted phishing, including malware, spoofing attacks, payload-less Business Email Compromise attacks, supply chain phishing, and other advanced threats.</p>
