<h2 id="email-evaluation">Email evaluation</h2>
<p>The scanning service and verdict engines used by Email security are language agnostic. We provide support for Double Byte Character sets (DBCS) and UTF-8, UTF-16, and UTF-32 encoding.</p>
<h2 id="dashboard">Dashboard</h2>
<p>The Email security email dashboard is localized to several languages. To update your language settings:</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/">Email security dashboard</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>At the bottom of the page, select the language icon.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p><img src="/assets/upstream/images/email-security/language-switcher.png" alt="Select the language icon to toggle your dashboard between English and Japanese." /></p>
</div>

<li>Select your preferred language.</li>
</ol>
