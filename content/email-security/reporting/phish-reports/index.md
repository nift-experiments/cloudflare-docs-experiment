<p>Email security automatically generates <div class="nb-interactive-component" data-cf-component="GlossaryTooltip"></p>
@markup("md", "content/.markup/bodies/8457.md")
</div> reports to provide an overview of your email traffic. The report only includes malicious emails. Spam and bulk emails are not included.
<h2 id="in-the-dashboard">In the dashboard</h2>
<p>To view phishing reports in the Email security dashboard, <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/">log in</a> and explore the non-Settings areas of the Email security dashboard (<strong>Home</strong>, <strong>Email</strong>, <strong>Web</strong>, and <strong>Detection Details</strong>).</p>
<h2 id="through-an-email-subscription">Through an email subscription</h2>
<p>The same reports that are visible through the dashboard can also be delivered through an email.</p>
<p>To subscribe an email address to daily or weekly reports:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/">Email security dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings</strong> (the gear icon).</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Subscriptions</strong> &gt; <strong>Email Subscriptions</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add Subscriber</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter an <strong>Email</strong> and choose the desired reports.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add Subscriber</strong>.</li>
</ol>
