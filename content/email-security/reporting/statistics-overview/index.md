<p>To access an overview of your account, total number of emails processed, a breakdown of types of threads detected, among other types of information:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/users/login">Email security dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Make sure you are in the Home section to review information regarding your account:</li>
</ol>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Field</th>
<th>Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>System stats</strong></td>
<td><ul><li>Status of Area 1’s services</li> <li>Uptime of Area 1’s services as well as any downtime</li> <li>Number of processed emails and attacks prevented</li></ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Detection stats</strong></td>
<td>Statistics regarding the total number of detections made, and emails processed.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Retractions</strong></td>
<td>Shows the distribution of messages removed from your user's mailboxes.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Phish Submissions Stats</strong></td>
<td>Statistics regarding the number of phish emails submitted by your users and security operations center (SOC)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Threat Origins</strong></td>
<td>Top geographical threat origins to your organization.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Org Spoofs</strong></td>
<td>Shows attacks where names in envelopes differ from the header, as well as spoofed domains.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Domain Proximity</strong></td>
<td>List of domains similar to your own.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Malicious Threat Type</strong></td>
<td>Breakdown of malicious threat types.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Email Link Isolation</strong></td>
<td>How many email were processed by <a href="/email-security/email-configuration/email-policies/link-actions/#email-link-isolation">Email Link Isolation</a>.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Top BEC Targets</strong></td>
<td>What email addresses are the top targets on the <a href="/email-security/email-configuration/enhanced-detections/business-email-compromise/">Business Email Compromise feature</a>.</td>
</tr>
