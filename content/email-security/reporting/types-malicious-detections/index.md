<p>To review the number and type of malicious detections made on your account:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/users/login">Email security dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Select the <strong>Email</strong> tab.</li>
<li>The <strong>Overview</strong> section will show you graphs with the total number of emails processed, as well as how many of those pertain to different threat categories - such as Malicious or Spam, among others. Refer to <a href="/email-security/reference/dispositions-and-attributes/">Dispositions and attributes</a> for more information. Select <strong>View Details</strong>.</li>
<li>You will open the <strong>Detections</strong> page. This page breaks down the information regarding the various types of threats detected. You have access to:</li>
</ol>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Field</th>
<th>Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>Attachments</strong></td>
<td><ul><li>How many of the malicious emails received have an attachment.</li> <li>Of these, what are the top types of malicious files received (for example, PDF files).</li></ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Senders</strong></td>
<td><ul><li>Total number of malicious senders, as well as a graph showing how they are distributed throughout the month.</li> <li>Top malicious domains.</li></ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Targets</strong></td>
<td>Top email targets on the <a href="/email-security/email-configuration/enhanced-detections/business-email-compromise/">BEC feature</a>.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>New domains</strong></td>
<td><ul><li>Total number of malicious domains registered in the past month.</li> <li>Most common top level malicious domains.</li></ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Links</strong></td>
<td><ul><li>Total number of malicious links and their distribution throughout the month.</li> <li>Top threat types (for example, credential harvester).</li></ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Threat types</strong></td>
<td>Top malicious threat types, and their percentage relatively to the total amount of threats received.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Threat origins</strong></td>
<td>A graph representing where in the world are your top threat origins.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
