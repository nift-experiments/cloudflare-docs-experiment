<p>To review the number and type of malicious detections made on your account:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the <a href="https://horizon.area1security.com/users/login">Email security dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Select the <strong>Email</strong> tab.</li>
<li>The <strong>Overview</strong> section will show you graphs with the total number of emails processed, as well as how many of those pertain to different threat categories - such as Malicious or Spam, among others. Refer to <a href="/email-security/reference/dispositions-and-attributes/">Dispositions and attributes</a> for more information. Select <strong>View Details</strong>.</li>
<li>You will open the <strong>Detections</strong> page. This page breaks down the information regarding the various types of threats detected. You have access to:</li>
</ol>
