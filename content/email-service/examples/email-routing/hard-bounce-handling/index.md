<h2 id="what-are-hard-bounces">What are hard bounces?</h2>
<p>Hard bounces occur when an email cannot be delivered due to permanent reasons:</p>
<ul>
<li>Invalid email address: The email address does not exist</li>
<li>Domain does not exist: The domain name is invalid or expired</li>
<li>Mailbox full: The recipient's mailbox has exceeded storage limits</li>
<li>Email blocked: The recipient's server permanently rejects emails</li>
</ul>
