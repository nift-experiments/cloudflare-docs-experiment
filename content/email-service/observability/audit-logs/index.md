<p>Email Service writes configuration changes to <a href="/fundamentals/account/account-security/review-audit-logs/">Cloudflare audit logs</a>. Use audit logs to track who changed what and when.</p>
<h2 id="email-routing-actions">Email Routing actions</h2>
<p>The following Email Routing actions are recorded:</p>
<ul>
<li>Add, edit, or delete a routing rule.</li>
<li>Add or delete a destination address.</li>
<li>Change the status of a destination address (for example, from pending to verified).</li>
<li>Update the catch-all rule.</li>
<li>Enable, disable, or unlock the zone for Email Routing.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="email-sending-actions">Email Sending actions</h2>
<p>The following Email Sending actions are recorded:</p>
<ul>
<li>Onboard or remove a sending domain or subdomain.</li>
<li>Add, edit, or delete entries on the suppression list.</li>
<li>Enable or disable Email Sending on a domain.</li>
</ul>
<p>To review audit logs, refer to <a href="/fundamentals/account/account-security/review-audit-logs/">Review audit logs</a>.</p>
