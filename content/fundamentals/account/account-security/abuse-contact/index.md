<p>Enter an abuse contact email address to ensure you are receiving communications regarding potential abuse on your websites.</p>
<p>To update your abuse contact email address:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log into the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com">Cloudflare dashboard</a> and select your account.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Manage Account</strong> &gt; <strong>Configurations</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Abuse report contact email address</strong>, select <strong>Change email address</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter and confirm your new email and select <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>If you choose not to provide an abuse contact email address, communication about abuse will be directed to one of the Super Administrators on your account.</p>
