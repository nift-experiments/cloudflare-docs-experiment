<p>Occasionally, you may want to allow edit access to your Account Team. A typical use case might be migrating a complex or sensitive domain over to Cloudflare.</p>
<p>By default, Cloudflare does not have edit access to your account.</p>
<p>To enable editing access by your Account Team:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Configurations</strong> page. (You must be logged in as a <strong>Super Administrator</strong>).</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>For <strong>Editing Permission</strong>, switch the toggle to <strong>On</strong>.</li>
<li>Select a duration.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Approve</strong>.</li>
</ol>
