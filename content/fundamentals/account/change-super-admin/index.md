<p>If you or someone in your organization leaves or loses access to email, you can add another Super Administrator using any other Super Administrator on your Account with a <a href="https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/account/verify-email-address/">verified email</a> address.</p>
<p>First, <a href="/fundamentals/manage-members/manage/">add a member</a> to your account and assign the <strong>Super Administrator</strong> role.</p>
<p>Then, if needed, remove the previous Super Administrator.</p>
