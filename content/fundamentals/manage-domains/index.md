<p>A <em>domain</em> or <em>domain name</em> (also known as a <em>zone</em>) is the location of a website or application, or what an end user types into their browser to get to your website (<code>example.com</code>).</p>
<h2 id="get-a-domain-name">Get a domain name</h2>
<p>You can purchase domain names for your website from a variety of places. Cloudflare offers an at-cost registrar service to <a href="/registrar/get-started/register-domain/">purchase new domain names</a> or <a href="/registrar/get-started/transfer-domain-to-cloudflare/">transfer existing domain names</a>.</p>
<p>Refer to <a href="/fundamentals/reference/best-practices/">Account and domain management best practices</a> for a detailed list of ways to protect your account and domain.</p>
<h2 id="host-your-domain">Host your domain</h2>
<p>A web host keeps your website online so visitors can reach it via the domain name.</p>
<p>Cloudflare does not offer web hosting for most websites, though you can deploy and host JAMstack sites with <a href="/pages/">Cloudflare Pages</a>.</p>
<h2 id="add-a-domain-to-cloudflare">Add a domain to Cloudflare</h2>
<p>For help onboarding a domain to Cloudflare's CDN, refer to our <a href="/fundamentals/manage-domains/add-site/">setup guide</a>.</p>
<p>You will need to <a href="/dns/zone-setups/full-setup/">update your domain's nameservers</a> and <a href="/dns/proxy-status/">proxy</a> your web traffic to benefit from caching, DDoS protection, Argo Smart Routing, and other <a href="/directory/?product-group=Application+performance%2CApplication+security">application security and performance products</a>.</p>
<h2 id="get-free-ssl-certificates">Get free SSL certificates</h2>
<p>Cloudflare offers free, unshared, publicly trusted <a href="/ssl/edge-certificates/universal-ssl/">Universal SSL certificates</a> to all Cloudflare domains.</p>

<p>For more details about subdomains (<code>www.example.com</code> or <code>blog.example.com</code>), refer to <a href="/fundamentals/manage-domains/manage-subdomains/">Manage subdomains</a>.</p>
