<p>For quick access to commonly configured domains (also known as &quot;zones&quot;), star up to ten domains per account in the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
<h2 id="star-a-domain">Star a domain</h2>
<p>To star a domain:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log into the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com">Cloudflare dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Select your account and domain.</li>
<li>On the website <strong>Overview</strong>, select <strong>Star</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p><img src="/assets/upstream/images/fundamentals/star-domain.png" alt="Star domain on the Overview page of the website" /></p>
<h2 id="filter-to-starred-domains">Filter to starred domains</h2>
<p>To view only starred domains in your account:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log into the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com">Cloudflare dashboard</a>.</li>
<li>Select your account.</li>
<li>On the account <strong>Home</strong>, select <strong>Starred</strong>.</li>
</ol>
