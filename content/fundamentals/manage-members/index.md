<p>On any Cloudflare account, you can collaborate by adding members to your account and assigning them access via one or several policies.</p>
<p>Configuring permissions for each member helps prevent accidental changes to your account. For example, you can scope a team member to only manage staging domains, so they do not accidentally modify a production site. Use policies to grant each member the minimum level of access they need.</p>
<p>Every policy has three parts:</p>
<ol>
<li>The actor</li>
<li>The role</li>
<li>The scope</li>
</ol>
<p>Refer to the resources below to configure policies to ensure that you can assign only the necessary access permissions to your account members.</p>
