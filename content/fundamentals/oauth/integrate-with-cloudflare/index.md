<p>After <a href="/fundamentals/oauth/create-an-oauth-client/">registering an application</a>, use these endpoints to integrate your OAuth client with Cloudflare.</p>
<ul>
<li>Jwks: <code>https://dash.cloudflare.com/.well-known/jwks.json</code></li>
<li>Open ID config: <code>https://dash.cloudflare.com/.well-known/openid-configuration</code></li>
<li>Authorization: <code>https://dash.cloudflare.com/oauth2/auth</code></li>
<li>Token: <code>https://dash.cloudflare.com/oauth2/token</code></li>
<li>Revoke: <code>https://dash.cloudflare.com/oauth2/revoke</code></li>
<li>Session logout: <code>https://dash.cloudflare.com/oauth2/logout</code></li>
<li>User info: <code>https://dash.cloudflare.com/oauth2/userinfo</code></li>
</ul>
