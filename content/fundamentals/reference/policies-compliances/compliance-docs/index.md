<p>Super Administrators can access common compliance documentation, such as PCI, SOC 2, ISO, and more, through the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
<p>To access compliance documentation:</p>
<ol>
<li>Visit <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/compliance-docs">Compliance Documents</a> and select your account where you are a <strong>Super Administrator</strong>.</li>
<li>If you have not accessed this page before, read the confidentiality statement and select <strong>I Agree</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the document you need and select <strong>Download</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="tax-documentation">Tax documentation</h2>
<p>Super Administrators, Billing Administrators, and Administrators can access tax documentation, such W-9 and Tax Certificates, through the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
<p>To access tax documentation:</p>
<ol>
<li>Visit <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/tax-docs">Tax Documents</a> and select your account where you are a <strong>Super Administrator</strong>, <strong>Billing Administrator</strong> or <strong>Administrator</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the document you need and select <strong>Download</strong>.</li>
</ol>
