<p>Use Cloudflare's <a href="https://abuse.cloudflare.com/">online abuse form</a> to report different types of abuse.</p>
<hr />
<h2 id="dmca-complaints">DMCA complaints</h2>
<p>Valid <a href="https://www.copyright.gov/dmca/">Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)</a> complaints must provide all of the following details:</p>
<ul>
<li>A physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is valid) of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.</li>
<li>Identification of the infringed copyright (for example, a link to your original work or clear description of the materials allegedly infringed upon).</li>
<li>Identification of the infringing material and information reasonably sufficient to allow Cloudflare to locate the material on the infringing website (for example, a <a href="/fundamentals/reference/report-abuse/provide-specific-urls/">link to the site</a> where the infringed copyrighted material appears).</li>
<li>Your contact information, including your address, telephone number, and email address.</li>
<li>A statement that you believe, in good faith, that the use of the material in the manner asserted is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.</li>
<li>A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.</li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h2 id="phishing">Phishing</h2>
<p>Valid phishing reports must provide all of the following details:</p>
<ul>
<li>The domain in question.</li>
<li>The specific link to the phishing page.</li>
</ul>
<p>After Cloudflare confirms existence of the phishing page, Cloudflare provides a warning page to visitors accessing the phishing link. Cloudflare also notifies the site owner to clean the malicious files from their origin web server.</p>
<hr />
<h2 id="trademark-infringement">Trademark infringement</h2>
<p>Cloudflare only acknowledges abuse reports from trademark holders or their legally authorized representatives.</p>
<p>For more details about what information is required, refer to <a href="https://abuse.cloudflare.com/">our abuse form</a>.</p>
<hr />
<h2 id="malware-sites">Malware sites</h2>
<p>Legitimate reports of malware URLs are blocked from loading via Cloudflare.</p>
<p>For more details about what information is required, refer to <a href="https://abuse.cloudflare.com/">our abuse form</a>.</p>
<hr />
<h2 id="child-exploitation-material">Child exploitation material</h2>
<p>Cloudflare promptly responds to all valid reports of child exploitation material. When Cloudflare is made aware of a website solely dedicated to the sharing or promotion of child exploitation material, the offending website is immediately removed from our network without notice.</p>
<p>For an expedited review, report child exploitation material via our <a href="https://abuse.cloudflare.com/">abuse form</a>.</p>
<p>Our Trust &amp; Safety team files a complaint with the <a href="http://www.missingkids.com/gethelpnow#onlinechildexploitation">National Center for Missing and Exploited Children</a> but suggest that you also file a complaint.</p>
