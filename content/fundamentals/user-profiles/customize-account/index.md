<p>From your Profile, you can modify settings that affect the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
<h2 id="language">Language</h2>
<p>Change the language used throughout the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the Cloudflare dashboard.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Go to <strong>Profile</strong>.</li>
<li>From <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Language</strong>, select a language.</li>
</ol>
<p>Your dashboard will update to the new language automatically.</p>
<h2 id="dashboard-appearance">Dashboard appearance</h2>
<p>Adjust how the Cloudflare dashboard appears on your device.</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the Cloudflare dashboard.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Go to <strong>Profile</strong>.</li>
<li>From <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Dashboard appearance</strong>, choose a value:
<ul>
<li><strong>Dark</strong>: Defaults to darker colors.</li>
<li><strong>Light</strong>: Defaults to lighter colors.</li>
<li><strong>Use system setting</strong>: Defaults to the option used on your device.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<p>Your dashboard display will update to the new appearance setting automatically.</p>
<h2 id="notifications">Notifications</h2>
<p>Choose the type of notifications you receive from Cloudflare, such as marketing announcements or insights about your domain.</p>
<p>To update the communication preferences for your profile (which requires a <a href="/fundamentals/user-profiles/verify-email-address/">verified email address)</a>:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the Cloudflare dashboard.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Go to <strong>Profile</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Notifications</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the categories of notifications you want to receive. Your choices are saved automatically.</li>
</ol>
<aside class="nb-aside note">

</aside>
<p>Refer to <a href="/notifications/">Cloudflare Notifications</a> to receive information about your account, such denial-of-service attacks or server issues.</p>
<h2 id="timezone">Timezone</h2>
<p>Choose to set the timezone in the Cloudflare dashboard as Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) or your browser or system's timezone.</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the Cloudflare dashboard.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select your <strong>Profile</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Set Timezone</strong> and choose either <strong>Standard (UTC)</strong> or <strong>Local (CST)</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>The page reloads to apply the new setting.</p>
