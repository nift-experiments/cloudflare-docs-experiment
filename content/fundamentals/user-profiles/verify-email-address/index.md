<p>For security reasons, Cloudflare attempts to verify the email address associated with your account. You cannot perform certain tasks within the Cloudflare dashboard -- for example, <a href="/fundamentals/manage-members/manage/#add-account-members">adding a new member</a>, <a href="/fundamentals/user-profiles/change-password-or-email/#change-email-address">changing your email address</a> or <a href="/fundamentals/user-profiles/customize-account/#notifications">updating your communication preferences</a> -- without verifying your email.</p>
<h2 id="when-creating-your-account">When creating your account</h2>
<p>When you first <a href="/fundamentals/account/create-account/">create an account</a>, Cloudflare automatically sends a message to the email address you provided for your account.</p>
<p>To verify your email:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to your email provider and find your recent message from Cloudflare. If you cannot find the message, check your Spam folder.</li>
<li>Go to the link in the email.</li>
<li>Log in to Cloudflare to verify the email address associated with your account.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="resend-verification-emails">Resend verification emails</h2>
<p>If you cannot find your verification email or your email has expired, request another verification email:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the Cloudflare dashboard.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Go to <strong>My Profile</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Email Address</strong>, select <strong>Send verification email</strong> (if this option is not available, your email has already been verified).</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="verification-issues">Verification issues</h2>
<p>If you experience issues with your verification link, you might have already verified your email address.</p>
<p>To check your verification:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log in to the Cloudflare dashboard.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Go to <strong>My Profile</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Email Address</strong>, your email address will have <code>(verified)</code> added after it.</li>
</ol>
<p>If your email is still not verified, try clicking the verification link in a different browser or a private window.</p>
<p>If this still does not work, try <a href="#resend-verification-emails">resending</a> the verification email to get a new verification link.</p>
