<p>Hyperdrive is a service that accelerates queries you make to existing databases, making it faster to access your data from across the globe from <a href="/workers/">Cloudflare Workers</a>, irrespective of your users' location.</p>
<p>Hyperdrive supports any Postgres or MySQL database, including those hosted on AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, Neon and PlanetScale. Hyperdrive also supports Postgres-compatible databases like CockroachDB and Timescale.
You do not need to write new code or replace your favorite tools: Hyperdrive works with your existing code and tools you use.</p>
<p>Use Hyperdrive's connection details from your Cloudflare Workers application with your existing database drivers and object-relational mapping (ORM) libraries.</p>
