<p>Cloudflare Tunnel allows you to securely connect your applications to Cloudflare without a publicly routable IP address. With Tunnel, you do not send traffic to an external IP — instead, a lightweight daemon in your infrastructure (<code>cloudflared</code>) creates outbound-only connections to Cloudflare's global network.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>By the end of this module, you will be able to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Create a Cloudflare Tunnel.</li>
<li>Publish your application via a public hostname.</li>
</ul>
