<p>To create a new Cloudflare account:</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up">Sign up</a> on the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To secure your account, enable <a href="/fundamentals/user-profiles/2fa/">two-factor authentication</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>After your account is accepted into the Cybersafe Schools program, you can to reach out to your Cloudflare contact and ask them to set up your account as a multi-user organization. This will allow you to define <a href="/fundamentals/manage-members/roles/">role based access</a> controls. For this project, account members will need:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="/cloudflare-one/roles-permissions/"><strong>Gateway</strong> permissions</a> to read or edit DNS policies.</li>
<li><a href="/cloudflare-one/roles-permissions/#cloudflare-zero-trust-pii"><strong>PII</strong> permissions</a> to view user information in DNS logs.</li>
<li><a href="/email-security/account-setup/permissions/"><strong>Configuration</strong> permissions</a> for Area 1 Email security.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
