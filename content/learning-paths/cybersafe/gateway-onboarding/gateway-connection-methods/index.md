<p>There are various methods to onboard your traffic and your method will be determined based on your individual or organizational requirements. In this learning path, we will focus on an <a href="/cloudflare-one/networks/resolvers-and-proxies/dns/"><strong>Agentless (DNS only) deployment method</strong></a>.</p>
<p>Protecting more than DNS, such as network packets, HTTP requests, or egress traffic is possible, but requires a more advanced deployment method such as <a href="/cloudflare-one/team-and-resources/devices/cloudflare-one-client/">WARP</a> or <a href="/cloudflare-wan/zero-trust/cloudflare-gateway/">Cloudflare WAN</a>.</p>
