<p>Once pre-flight checks are completed, Cloudflare unlocks your <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="prefix">prefixes</span> for you to <a href="/magic-transit/how-to/advertise-prefixes/">advertise via the dashboard, API or BGP</a> at a time of your choosing. Refer to <a href="/byoip/concepts/dynamic-advertisement/best-practices/">Dynamic advertisement best practices</a> to learn more about advertising prefixes.</p>
<p>If you are using a Cloudflare IP, you do not need to advertise your prefixes.</p>
