<p>In this unit you will learn how to set up tunnels and routes to steer traffic.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>By the end of this module you will be able to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Create tunnels on both the Cloudflare side and your router side to connect to your infrastructure.</li>
<li>Configure static routes or dynamic routes with BGP peering to steer your traffic via next-hop from Cloudflare's global network to your connected networks.</li>
</ul>
