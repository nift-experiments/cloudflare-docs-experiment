<p>Magic Transit customers are automatically provided with the <a href="/cloudflare-network-firewall/plans/#standard-features">standard features</a> of Cloudflare Network Firewall, Cloudflare's firewall-as-a-service product.</p>
<p>Cloudflare recommends creating a ruleset customized to your environment and needs. Without any rules configured, Cloudflare Network Firewall will pass on all traffic after mitigations are applied to your tunnels.</p>
<p>The <a href="/cloudflare-network-firewall/best-practices/extended-ruleset/">Extended ruleset</a> is the best practice for reducing your attack surface by adopting a positive security model. If possible, use your current Edge Firewall policies to help you decide what ports to permit/block.</p>
<p>If you cannot use the extended ruleset, then use the <a href="/cloudflare-network-firewall/best-practices/minimal-ruleset/">minimal ruleset guidance</a> to create a customized ruleset to block known unwanted traffic and common vectors for attack.</p>
