<p>Migrating your DNS from an on-premises BIND solution to a cloud-based provider like Cloudflare can offer significant benefits in terms of performance, security, and manageability. However, a successful migration requires careful planning and execution. This guide outlines best practices to ensure a smooth transition.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>By the end of this module, you will be able to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Plan and take inventory of your DNS migration.</li>
<li>Prepare for the migration with minimal downtime.</li>
<li>Learn how to make the actual switch to Cloudflare.</li>
<li>Verify and stabilize after your migration.</li>
</ul>
