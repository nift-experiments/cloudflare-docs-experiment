<p>Learn how to customize and augment Cloudflare’s DDoS protection.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>By the end of this module, you will be able to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Audit DNS records to prevent accidental IP address exposures.</li>
<li>Optimize caching and security to reduce incoming traffic (whether malicious or legitimate).</li>
<li>Augment Cloudflare's default analytics to examine attacks in more detail.</li>
<li>Evaluate different ways to restrict external connections to your origin server.</li>
</ul>
