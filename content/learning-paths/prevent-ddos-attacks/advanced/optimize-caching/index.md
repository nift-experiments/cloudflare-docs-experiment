<p>The more content is cached, the fewer requests go back to your origin server (whether due to legitimate or illegitimate traffic).</p>
<p>A few ways to optimize Cloudflare caching include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Creating <a href="/cache/how-to/cache-rules/">cache rules</a> to customize the cache properties of specific HTTP requests.</li>
<li>Enabling the <a href="/cache/how-to/tiered-cache/">Tiered Cache</a> feature, which dramatically increases cache hit ratios.</li>
<li>Reviewing our other various <a href="/cache/get-started/">configuration options</a>, which may vary based on your plan and application setup.</li>
</ul>
