<p>Once you <a href="/learning-paths/prevent-ddos-attacks/baseline/proxy-dns-records/">proxy your DNS records</a>, you should enable rulesets for Cloudflare's <a href="/waf/">Web Application Firewall (WAF)</a>.</p>
<p>The available rulesets depend on your zone's plan, but all customers have access at least to the Cloudflare Free Managed Ruleset, which provides mitigations against high and wide-impacting vulnerabilities.</p>
<p>For more details and potential customizations, refer to <a href="/waf/managed-rules/">Managed rulesets</a>.</p>
