<p>This module covers how to connect your private network services and applications to Cloudflare. In many ways, this connection will replace the concept of a traditional VPN concentrator or headend device.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>By the end of this module, you will be able to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Create a Cloudflare Tunnel.</li>
<li>Configure private network routes for your tunnel.</li>
<li>Understand how to size and scale your tunnel.</li>
</ul>
