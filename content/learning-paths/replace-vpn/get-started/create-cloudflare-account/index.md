<p>To create a new Cloudflare account:</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up">Sign up</a> on the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To secure your account, enable <a href="/fundamentals/user-profiles/2fa/">two-factor authentication</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you have a Cloudflare contact (Enterprise only), ask them to set up your account as a multi-user organization. Account members will need:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="/cloudflare-one/roles-permissions/"><strong>Access</strong> permissions</a> to read or edit applications and Access policies.</li>
<li><a href="/cloudflare-one/roles-permissions/"><strong>Gateway</strong> permissions</a> to read or edit Gateway policies.</li>
<li><a href="/cloudflare-one/roles-permissions/#cloudflare-zero-trust-pii"><strong>PII</strong> permissions</a> to view user information in Gateway activity logs.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="best-practices">Best practices</h2>
<p>If you are creating an account for your team or a business, we recommend choosing an email alias or distribution list for your <strong>Email</strong>, such as <code>cloudflare@example.com</code>.</p>
<p>This email address is the main point of contact for your Cloudflare billing, usage notifications, and account recovery.</p>
