<p>In the context of DNS filtering, a blocklist is a list of known harmful domains or IP addresses. An allowlist is a list of allowed domains or IP addresses, such as the domains of essential corporate applications.</p>
<p>Gateway supports creating <a href="/cloudflare-one/reusable-components/lists/">lists</a> of URLs, hostnames, or other entries to use in your policies.</p>
