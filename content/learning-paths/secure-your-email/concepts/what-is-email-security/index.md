<p>Despite email's importance as a communication method, security and privacy were not built into the <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/email-security/what-is-smtp/">The Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) protocol</a>. As a result, email is a major attack vector.</p>
<p>Email security is the process of preventing <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/email-security/what-is-email/">email-based</a> cyber attacks and unwanted communications. It spans protecting inboxes from takeover, protecting domains from <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/ssl/what-is-domain-spoofing/">spoofing</a>, stopping <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/access-management/phishing-attack/">phishing attacks</a>, preventing fraud, blocking <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/ddos/glossary/malware/">malware</a> delivery, and filtering <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/email-security/how-to-stop-spam-emails/">spam</a>.</p>
