<p>Now that you have set up your email environment, you can enable auto-move events.</p>
<aside class="nb-aside caution">
@markup("md", "content/.markup/bodies/10272.md")
</aside>
<p>Auto-move events are events where emails are automatically moved to different inboxes based on the disposition assigned to them by Email security.</p>
<p>When you set up auto-moves, you can move messages manually or set up automatic moves to send messages matching certain <a href="/learning-paths/secure-your-email/enable-auto-moves/email-dispositions/">dispositions</a> to specific folders within a user's mailbox.</p>
