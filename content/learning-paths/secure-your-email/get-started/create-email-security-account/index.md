<p>To create your Email security account, you will need the alphanumeric string on the URL when logged in to the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
<p>If you do not have a Cloudflare account, you can create one for free by referring to the <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up">Cloudflare sign-up page</a>.</p>
<p>Once you have created your account, your account team will create an Email security account for you.</p>
<p>To establish your tenant, you will need the following information:</p>
<ul>
<li>Average monthly inbound message volume</li>
<li>Number of active email users</li>
<li>At least one domain</li>
<li>Admin email address</li>
</ul>
