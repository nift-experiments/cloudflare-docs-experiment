<ol>
<li>Log in to <a href="https://one.dash.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare One</a>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Email security</strong> &gt; <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Connect an integration</strong>, choose <strong>Google Workspace CASB + EMAIL</strong>, then select <strong>Select Integration</strong>.</li>
<li>Follow the wizard to create an integration, then select <strong>Create Integration</strong>. Refer to <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/setup/post-delivery-deployment/bcc-journaling/bcc-setup/gmail-bcc-setup/enable-gmail-integration/">Enable Gmail BCC integration</a> for further instructions.</li>
