<p><a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/phishguard/">PhishGuard</a> serves as an extension of your Security Operations team with dedicated Email security technical resources providing real-time monitoring of your email environment. The Active Defense Service provides:</p>
<ul>
<li>Customized notification and responses for fraud and insider threats.</li>
<li>Reclassification of messages if the disposition is incorrect.</li>
<li>PhishGuard monitors and reviews Suspicious email traffic.</li>
<li>Quarantine and auto-move of identified threats.</li>
<li>Tailored threat hunting for your email environment.</li>
<li>Custom detections.</li>
</ul>
<p>As a PhishGuard customer, the following service offerings should be enabled:</p>
<ul>
<li>Escalation contacts must be configured in the Email security dashboard: This allows for email reports to be delivered regarding high risk items identified and responded to by the team.</li>
<li>Auto-moves should be enabled and configured for quarantine of identified items: Malicious should be prioritized, but configuring Spam for a move to junk/trash or even soft delete may also be highly useful to the client.</li>
</ul>
<p>Refer to the <a href="/cloudflare-one/email-security/phishguard/">PhishGuard</a> documentation to learn more about this add-on service.</p>
