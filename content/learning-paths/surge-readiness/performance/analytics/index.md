<h2 id="workers-analytics-engine">Workers Analytics Engine</h2>
<p>Use the <a href="/analytics/analytics-engine/">Workers Analytics Engine</a> to send unlimited-cardinality data from your Worker to a time-series database. Query it with SQL.</p>
<h2 id="account-and-zone-analytics">Account and zone analytics</h2>
<p>Use <a href="/analytics/account-and-zone-analytics/">Account and zone analytics</a> to provide details about the requests and traffic related to your Cloudflare accounts and zones.</p>
<h2 id="cloudflare-network-analytics">Cloudflare Network Analytics</h2>
<p>Use <a href="/analytics/network-analytics/">Cloudflare Network Analytics</a> to provide near real-time visibility into network and transport-layer traffic patterns and DDoS attacks.</p>
<h2 id="graphql-analytics-api">GraphQL Analytics API</h2>
<p>Use the <a href="/analytics/graphql-api/">GraphQL Analytics API</a> to provide\ all of your performance, security, and reliability data from one endpoint. Select exactly what you need, from one metric for a domain to multiple metrics aggregated for your account.</p>
