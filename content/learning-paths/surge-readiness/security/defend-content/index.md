<p>Scrape Shield is a collection of settings meant to protect your site's content.</p>
<h2 id="email-address-obfuscation">Email Address Obfuscation</h2>
<p><a href="/waf/tools/scrape-shield/email-address-obfuscation/">Email Address Obfuscation</a> uses JavaScript to encrypt addresses and prevents harvesting by spammers and bots while keeping addresses easy to read and use for human visitors.</p>
<h2 id="hotlink-protection">Hotlink Protection</h2>
<p><a href="/waf/tools/scrape-shield/hotlink-protection/">Hotlink Protection</a> prevents your images from being used by other sites, which can reduce the bandwidth consumed by your origin server.</p>
