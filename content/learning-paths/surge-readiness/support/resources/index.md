<h2 id="additional-resources">Additional resources</h2>
<ul>
<li>For help with an issue, refer to <a href="/support/contacting-cloudflare-support/">guidance for submitting support tickets</a>.</li>
<li>Reference our <a href="/support/">Support Docs</a>, including <a href="/support/contacting-cloudflare-support/#priority-definitions">Priority definitions</a>.</li>
<li>Learn the basic countermeasures to <a href="/ddos-protection/best-practices/proactive-defense/">prevent an ongoing DDoS attack</a>.</li>
<li>Let <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/soc-as-a-service/">Cloudflare's Security Operations Center-as-a-Service (SOC)</a> monitor your environment for volumetric security threats and potential operational disruptions, perform analysis to identify attack vectors, and help you implement countermeasures to mitigate future incidents.</li>
<li>If a customer has purchased Technical Account Management Service, utilize the <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/technical-account-management-service/">Technical Account Management Service</a> which operates as an extension of your team, as the Cloudflare support expert who knows your tech stack, unique infrastructure, and Cloudflare portfolio requirements.</li>
<li>Learn <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/whats-new/">what's new</a> and subscribe to product release email summaries.</li>
<li>Read the <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare blog</a> for the latest announcements from Cloudflare.</li>
