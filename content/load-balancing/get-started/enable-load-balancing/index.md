<p>Load balancing is an add-on for your account, meaning your account needs a <a href="/billing/get-started/create-billing-profile/">billing profile</a>.</p>
<p>To enable <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/:zone/traffic/load-balancing">Load Balancing</a>:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Load Balancing</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select <strong>Enable Load Balancing</strong> in the <strong>Status</strong> column.</li>
<li>Choose your plan options and confirm payment.</li>
</ol>
