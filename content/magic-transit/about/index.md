<p>Magic Transit is a network security and performance solution that offers Distributed Denial of Service (<a href="/ddos-protection/">DDoS</a>) protection, traffic acceleration, and more for on-premise, cloud-hosted, and hybrid networks.</p>
<p>Magic Transit delivers its connectivity, security, and performance benefits by serving as the front door to your IP network. This means it accepts IP <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="data packet">packets</span> destined for your network, processes them, and then outputs them to your origin infrastructure.</p>
<p>The Cloudflare network uses <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/security/glossary/what-is-bgp/">Border Gateway Protocol (BGP)</a> to announce your company's IP address space, extending your network presence globally, and <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="anycast">anycast</span> to ingest your traffic. Today, Cloudflare's anycast global network spans <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/network/">hundreds of cities worldwide</a>.</p>
<p>Once <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/network-layer/what-is-a-packet/">packets</a> hit Cloudflare's network, Cloudflare inspects traffic for attacks, filters, <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="traffic steering">steers</span>, accelerates, and sends it to your origin. Magic Transit connects to your origin infrastructure using anycast <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="GRE tunnel">Generic Routing Encapsulation (GRE)</span> tunnels over the Internet or, with <a href="/network-interconnect/">Cloudflare Network Interconnect (CNI)</a>, through physical or virtual interconnect.</p>
<p>You have two options for your Magic Transit implementation: ingress traffic or ingress and <a href="/magic-transit/reference/egress/">egress traffic</a>. With an egress implementation, you must set up <span class="nb-glossary-tooltip" title="policy-based routing">policy-based routing (PBR)</span> or ensure default routing on your end forwards traffic to Cloudflare through tunnels.</p>
