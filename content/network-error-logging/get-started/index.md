<p>Network Error Logging is available to users on all plan types.</p>
<p>To enable Network Error Logging for Free and Pro zones:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Account home</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select <strong>Network</strong> and locate <strong>Network Error Logging Monitoring</strong>.</li>
<li>Select the toggle to enable Network Error Logging.</li>
</ol>
<p>To enable this for Business and Enterprise, contact support or your account team.</p>
