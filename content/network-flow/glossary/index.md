<p>Review the definitions for terms used across Cloudflare's Network Flow (formerly Magic Network Monitoring) documentation.</p>
<table id="glossary-table"><thead><tr><th>Term</th><th>Definition</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>flow data</td><td><p>Represents records of communication between devices. There are a number of flow data protocols, such as NetFlow or sFlow.</p></td></tr><tr><td>NetFlow</td><td><p>Network protocol developed by Cisco to collect and monitor network traffic flow data.</p></td></tr><tr><td>sampling</td><td><p>In the context of Network Flow, sampling is the process of taking samples of packets for a specific period to identify potential attacks.</p></td></tr><tr><td>sFlow</td><td><p>An industry standard packet sampling protocol to monitor network devices.</p></td></tr></tbody></table>
