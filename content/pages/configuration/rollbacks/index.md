<p>Rollbacks allow you to instantly revert your project to a previous production deployment.</p>
<p>Any production deployment that has been successfully built is a valid rollback target. When your project has rolled back to a previous deployment, you may still rollback to deployments that are newer than your current version. Note that preview deployments are not valid rollback targets.</p>
<p>In order to perform a rollback, go to <strong>Deployments</strong> in your Pages project. Browse the <strong>All deployments</strong> list and select the three dotted actions menu for the desired target. Select <strong>Rollback to this deployment</strong> for a confirmation window to appear. When confirmed, your project's production deployment will change instantly.</p>
