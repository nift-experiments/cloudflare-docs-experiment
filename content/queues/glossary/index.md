<p>Review the definitions for terms used across Cloudflare's Queues documentation.</p>
<table id="glossary-table"><thead><tr><th>Term</th><th>Definition</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>consumer</td><td><p>A consumer is the term for a client that is subscribing to or consuming messages from a queue.</p></td></tr><tr><td>producer</td><td><p>A producer is the term for a client that is publishing or producing messages on to a queue.</p></td></tr><tr><td>queue</td><td><p>A queue is a buffer or list that automatically scales as messages are written to it, and allows a consumer Worker to pull messages from that same queue.</p></td></tr></tbody></table>
