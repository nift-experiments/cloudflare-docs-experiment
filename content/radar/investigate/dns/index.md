<p>Access aggregated and anonymized DNS queries to Cloudflare's <a href="/1.1.1.1/">1.1.1.1</a> public resolver service.</p>
<h2 id="list-of-endpoints">List of endpoints</h2>
<h3 id="top-locations">Top locations</h3>
<h4 id="example-geographical-distribution-of-google-com-versus-yandex-ru">Example: Geographical distribution of <code>google.com</code> versus <code>yandex.ru</code></h4>
<p>In the next example, we will request the top originating locations for <code>google.com</code> DNS queries:</p>
<pre><code class="language-bash">curl &quot;https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/radar/dns/top/locations?domain=google.com&amp;dateRange=1d&amp;format=json&amp;limit=2&quot; \&#10;&#45;-header &quot;Authorization: Bearer &lt;API_TOKEN&gt;&quot;&#10;</code></pre>
<p>The response shows that most queries come from the United States and Brazil:</p>
<pre><code class="language-json">{&#10;  &quot;success&quot;: true,&#10;  &quot;errors&quot;: [],&#10;  &quot;result&quot;: {&#10;		&quot;top_0&quot;: [&#10;			{&#10;				&quot;clientCountryAlpha2&quot;: &quot;US&quot;,&#10;				&quot;clientCountryName&quot;: &quot;United States&quot;,&#10;				&quot;value&quot;: &quot;43.474518&quot;&#10;			},&#10;			{&#10;				&quot;clientCountryAlpha2&quot;: &quot;BR&quot;,&#10;				&quot;clientCountryName&quot;: &quot;Brazil&quot;,&#10;				&quot;value&quot;: &quot;10.772799&quot;&#10;			}&#10;		],&#10;		&quot;meta&quot;: {&#10;      // ...&#10;    }&#10;  }&#10;}&#10;</code></pre>
<p>Making the same search request for <code>yandex.ru</code>, a Russian search engine:</p>
<pre><code class="language-bash">curl &quot;https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/radar/dns/top/locations?domain=yandex.ru&amp;dateRange=1d&amp;format=json&amp;limit=2&quot; \&#10;&#45;-header &quot;Authorization: Bearer &lt;API_TOKEN&gt;&quot;&#10;</code></pre>
<p>Returns the following response:</p>
<pre><code class="language-json">{&#10;  &quot;success&quot;: true,&#10;  &quot;errors&quot;: [],&#10;  &quot;result&quot;: {&#10;		&quot;top_0&quot;: [&#10;			{&#10;				&quot;clientCountryAlpha2&quot;: &quot;RU&quot;,&#10;				&quot;clientCountryName&quot;: &quot;Russian Federation&quot;,&#10;				&quot;value&quot;: &quot;73.710495&quot;&#10;			},&#10;			{&#10;				&quot;clientCountryAlpha2&quot;: &quot;DE&quot;,&#10;				&quot;clientCountryName&quot;: &quot;Germany&quot;,&#10;				&quot;value&quot;: &quot;5.518052&quot;&#10;			}&#10;		],&#10;		&quot;meta&quot;: {&#10;      // ...&#10;    }&#10;  }&#10;}&#10;</code></pre>
<p>As expected, most queries come from Russia.</p>
