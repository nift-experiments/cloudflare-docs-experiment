<h2 id="registration-requirements-for-us-domains">Registration requirements for .US domains</h2>
<p>If you want to register a <code>.us</code> domain, you must have a genuine connection to the United States as described in the <a href="https://www.about.us/policies">usTLD Nexus Policy</a>. When registering a domain name, registrants must identify the category under which they qualify for the usTLD Nexus Requirement:</p>

<h3 id="application-purpose">Application purpose</h3>
<p>In addition to nexus information, registrants must also identify their intended use of the domain name. The possible options are:</p>
<ul>
<li>Personal use</li>
<li>For-profit business</li>
<li>Non-profit business or organization</li>
<li>Government</li>
<li>Education</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="us-whois-requirements">.US WHOIS requirements</h3>
<p>The .US registry requires that domain contact data is displayed in the public WHOIS database. Redaction and/or use of WHOIS privacy services is prohibited. This is a registry policy that all registrars must comply with.</p>
<h3 id="us-domain-transfers">.US domain transfers</h3>
<p>Transferring a <code>.us</code> domain works in a similar way to other domains, but always requires approval via the Form of Authorization (FOA) email. You must select the approve link within five days for the transfer to proceed. If you do not respond, the transfer request will be cancelled.</p>
<p>Refer to <a href="/registrar/get-started/transfer-domain-to-cloudflare/">Transfer your domain to Cloudflare</a> for more information.</p>
