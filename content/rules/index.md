<p>Cloudflare Rules allows you to control how Cloudflare handles traffic to your website. For example, redirecting visitors, rewriting URLs, overriding where requests are sent, or customizing Cloudflare settings for specific requests.</p>
<p>Rules features require that your domain (or subdomain) has its <a href="/dns/proxy-status/">DNS records proxied</a> through Cloudflare, meaning traffic passes through the Cloudflare network before reaching your origin server.</p>
