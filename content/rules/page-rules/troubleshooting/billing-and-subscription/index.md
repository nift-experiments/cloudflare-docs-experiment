<h2 id="if-i-run-out-of-page-rules-can-i-buy-more">If I run out of Page Rules, can I buy more?</h2>
<p>No, you cannot buy additional Page Rules. You will need to <a href="/rules/reference/page-rules-migration/">migrate to modern Rules features</a> to benefit from increased quotas.</p>

<h2 id="when-i-change-plans-will-i-keep-my-add-on-subscriptions">When I change plans will I keep my add-on subscriptions?</h2>
<p>When you change plans, you will keep your Page Rules add-on subscription.</p>
<h2 id="what-happens-if-i-cancel-page-rules-halfway-through-my-billing-cycle">What happens if I cancel Page Rules halfway through my billing cycle?</h2>
<p>You will be billed for and have access to your additional Page Rules until the end of your current billing cycle.</p>
<p>Your plan will adjust at the start of your next billing cycle. For more details, refer to our <a href="/billing/understand/billing-policy/">billing policy</a>.</p>
