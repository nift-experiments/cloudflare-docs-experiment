<h1 id="cf-bot-management-score">cf.bot_management.score</h1>

**Data type:** Number

<p>Represents the likelihood that a request originates from a bot using a score from 1–99.</p>

<p>A low score indicates that the request comes from a bot or an automated agent. A high score indicates that a human issued the request.</p>
<p>Requires a Cloudflare Enterprise plan with <a href="/bots/plans/bm-subscription/">Bot Management</a> enabled.</p>
