<p>Cloudflare Secrets Store is a secure, centralized location in which account-level secrets are stored and managed. The secrets are securely encrypted and stored across all <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/network/">Cloudflare data centers</a>.</p>
<p>Secrets Store is currently compatible with <a href="/secrets-store/integrations/workers/">Cloudflare Workers</a> and <a href="/ai-gateway/configuration/bring-your-own-keys/">AI Gateway</a>. Integrations with other products will be added in the future.</p>
