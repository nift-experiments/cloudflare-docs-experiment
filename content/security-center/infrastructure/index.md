<p>The <strong>Infrastructure</strong> tab provides an overview of the IT assets associated with your Cloudflare account, including domains, IP addresses, and related configurations.</p>
<p><a href="/security/security-insights/">Security Insights</a> scans populate Infrastructure data. Initial scan time depends on the number of IT assets across the domains in your account.</p>
<p>To open the <strong>Infrastructure</strong> tab, go to Account Home &gt; <strong>Security Center</strong> &gt; <strong>Infrastructure</strong>.</p>
<p>From the Infrastructure tab, you can:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Filter the displayed information</strong> — Narrow results by specific assets, domains, or configurations to focus on areas of interest.</li>
<li><strong>Print or download a PDF report</strong> — Generate a report of your infrastructure overview for offline review or sharing with your team.</li>
<li><strong>Manage your security.txt file</strong> — Create or update a <a href="/security-center/infrastructure/security-file/"><code>security.txt</code></a> file that provides security researchers with a standardized way to report vulnerabilities.</li>
</ul>
