<p>Stream provides server-side analytics that can be used to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Identify most viewed video content in your app or platform.</li>
<li>Identify where content is viewed from and when it is viewed.</li>
<li>Understand which creators on your platform are publishing the most viewed content, and analyze trends.</li>
</ul>
<p>You can access data on either:</p>
<ul>
<li>The Stream <strong>Analytics</strong> page of the Cloudflare dashboard.</li>
</ul>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ul>
<li>The <a href="/stream/getting-analytics/fetching-bulk-analytics">GraphQL Analytics API</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>Users will need the <strong>Analytics</strong> permission to access analytics via Dash or GraphQL.</p>
