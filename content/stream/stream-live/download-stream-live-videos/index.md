<p>You can enable downloads for live stream videos from the Cloudflare dashboard. Videos are available for download after they enter the <strong>Ready</strong> state.</p>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
@markup("md", "content/.markup/bodies/14403.md")
</aside>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Live inputs</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Select a live input from the list.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Videos created by live input</strong>, select your video.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Settings</strong>, select <strong>Enable MP4 Downloads</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Save</strong>. You will see a progress bar as the video generates a download link.</li>
<li>When the download link is ready, under <strong>Download URL</strong>, copy the URL and enter it in a browser to download the video.</li>
</ol>
