<h2 id="check-video-status">Check video status</h2>
<p>Stream must download and encode the video, which can take a few seconds to a few minutes depending on the length of your video.</p>
<p>When the <code>readyToStream</code> value returns <code>true</code>, your video is ready for streaming.</p>
