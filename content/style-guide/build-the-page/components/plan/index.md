<p>The plan component lets you define what type of plan your product needs. Use it in the main <a href="/style-guide/documentation-content-strategy/content-types/overview/">Overview page</a> of your product, or in a page where you need to talk about a specific feature with additional requirements.</p>
<p>Use <code>&lt;Plan type=&quot;&lt;PLAN_TYPE&gt;&quot; /&gt;</code>, choosing the type that matches your product's availability.</p>
