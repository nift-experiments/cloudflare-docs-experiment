<p>This section shares how Cloudflare uses AI to accelerate and augment our content operations. We view AI as a tool that enables us to do our best work, faster. Whether we are designing prompts, researching a new product, or finding ways to turn a manual, week-long process into a job that takes an afternoon to complete, we are continuously looking for ways to iterate and streamline our operations. We know that when we can save time on one time-intensive task, we can spend more time on improving our content experiences for our customers.</p>
<p>As a result, we use and have used AI to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Vibecode, test, and deploy a web application for scoring in-product strings, error messages, and API docs.</li>
<li>Perform competitive analyses and audits on documentation.</li>
<li>Streamline documenting REST API examples.</li>
<li>Design prompts based on our content types, templates, and style to enable stakeholders with a doc idea to quickly draft content for us to review and publish.</li>
<li>Find topics missing descriptions, generate descriptions based on the page’s content, and add them to each page.</li>
<li>And more…</li>
</ul>
