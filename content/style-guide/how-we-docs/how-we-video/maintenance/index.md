<p>Producing a video is just the beginning. Videos are inherently harder to edit than text, making maintenance more complex. Even small changes often require re-recording, re-timing, or adjusting visuals. This makes it critical to ensure script accuracy and alignment with documentation from the start.</p>
<p>To keep videos up-to-date, we follow a simplified, two-pronged approach:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>AI-Assisted Periodic Checks</strong>: Upload videos to Gemini for automated checks against documentation and dashboard UI.</p>
<ul>
<li>Detect outdated workflows, UI elements, or inconsistencies.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Internal Feedback &amp; Updates</strong>: Rely on product managers (PMs), subject matter experts (SMEs), and internal signals when UI or functionality changes.</p>
<ul>
<li>Update scripts, visuals, or narration as needed and re-upload the video.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p>We are currently developing an internal tool for code-driven screen demos. When product code updates, the tool automatically updates video footage, minimizing manual re-recording and ensuring tutorials and demos reflect the latest product state.</p>
