<p>We believe video is a powerful tool that complements documentation to help users quickly grasp complex concepts and workflows. Not every piece of documentation should be a video and we should use it intentionally. Our goal is to apply video where it adds clarity, reduces cognitive load, and improves task completion to the original documentation.</p>
<p>We use video when it:</p>
<ul>
<li>Explains complex workflows</li>
<li>Demonstrates UI-driven actions</li>
<li>Reduces friction in onboarding and first-time use</li>
<li>Clarifies abstract or conceptual topics</li>
</ul>
<p>We do not use video for:</p>
<ul>
<li>Reference material (API fields, parameters, limits)</li>
<li>Frequently changing UI or experimental features</li>
<li>Content that is faster to scan in text</li>
<li>Information users need to copy, paste, or search precisely</li>
</ul>
