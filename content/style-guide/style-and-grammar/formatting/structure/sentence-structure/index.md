<p>Fifteen words or fewer is a good rule of thumb for readability. You can use the <a href="https://hemingwayapp.com/">Hemingway App</a> to check length and reading level. Break up longer or difficult-to-read sentences into shorter chunks.</p>
<p>In the Cloudflare dashboard, keep only what is necessary to convey the message. For example, complete sentences and certain words (&quot;the,&quot; for example) are not always necessary.</p>
