<h2 id="whitespace">Whitespace</h2>
<p>Whitespace is used in our blog posts and product content. For the sake of consistency, follow these conventions when using them:</p>
<table>
<tr>
<th style="width:20%">✅</th>
<th style="width:20%">❌</th>
<th>Example</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>nameserver</td>
<td>name servers</td>
<td>Change your nameservers to Cloudflare's.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>hostname</td>
<td>host name</td>
<td>Add a *. SAN (wildcard) to the custom hostname certificate.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>data center</td>
<td>datacenter</td>
<td>Cloudflare updates the new setting across all of our data centers around the world.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>checkbox</td>
<td>check box</td>
<td>Select the Transfer lock checkbox before continuing the setup.</td>
</tr>
</table>
<p>When using compound words that have a preposition in them (think log in, or set up), they should be written as two separate words when they are verbs, and as one word when they are nouns.</p>
<p>See these examples:</p>
<h3 id="verbs">Verbs</h3>
<p>Now you can <strong>set up</strong> Centrify, OneLogin, and other identity providers with Cloudflare Access.</p>
<p>Learn how to start using SSO to <strong>log in</strong> to the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
<p>Make sure to <strong>back up</strong> your data.</p>
<h3 id="nouns">Nouns</h3>
<p>Having DAGs that can run simultaneously requires an Airflow <strong>setup</strong> that has been built and configured to scale.</p>
<p>Share an account without sharing a <strong>login</strong>.</p>
<p>This allows you to restore your data from the <strong>backup</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="hyphenation">Hyphenation</h2>
<p>Certain expressions should be hyphenated depending on their position and role in the sentence.</p>
<p>As a general rule, when a compound word precedes a noun, it is called a <strong>modifier</strong> and should be hyphenated.
When the same word comes after a verb, it has the role of an adverb and it should not be hyphenated.</p>
<p>Examples:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Modifiers</th>
<th>Adverbs</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Keep <strong>up-to-date</strong> passwords secured and safe.</td>
<td>Keep passwords safe by staying <strong>up to date</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Day-to-day</strong> maintenance means any daily processes which must be implemented for the equipment.</td>
<td>The duration of a maintenance session will vary from <strong>day to day</strong>.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>This allows us to implement <strong>state-of-the-art</strong> security for our customers.</td>
<td>This approach to authentication was the <strong>state of the art</strong> until a couple years ago.</td>
</tr>
