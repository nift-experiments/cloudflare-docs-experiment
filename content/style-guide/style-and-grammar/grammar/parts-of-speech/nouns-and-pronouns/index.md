<p>Refer to the customer in the second person. Where this is not possible, use gender neutral pronouns. Do not use “one” as a pronoun.</p>
<p>Make sure it is clear who or what pronouns are in reference to. Pronouns act as signposts to the subject of the sentence and should not be used so much that the subject is no longer clear.</p>
<h2 id="first-person">First person</h2>
<p>Substitute &quot;we&quot; or &quot;I&quot; with &quot;Cloudflare&quot; if you need to represent Cloudflare. You do not need to use &quot;we&quot; constantly. However, use it when it is the best choice grammatically.</p>

<h2 id="second-person">Second person</h2>
<p>When you give instructions, use the polite command form with the second person &quot;you&quot; or in special cases &quot;they&quot; or &quot;it&quot;.</p>
<h2 id="referring-to-people">Referring to people</h2>
<p>Use &quot;you&quot; instead of &quot;the user&quot; when the audience is the user of a Cloudflare product.</p>
<p>As a last resort, use passive voice if the subject is obvious or irrelevant.</p>
