<p>Use the Oxford comma in lists of three or more items in a sentence.</p>
<p>Use commas to separate clauses. Break up longer or difficult-to-read sentences into shorter chunks.</p>
<p>Dependent clauses that start a sentence, end with a comma. <a href="https://www.dailywritingtips.com/subordinate-clauses-and-commas/">(Learn more)</a></p>
<p>Example: After you open the file, save it to your desktop with a different name.</p>
<h2 id="oxford-comma">Oxford comma</h2>
<p>Use the Oxford comma. The Oxford comma should break up lists of three or more items in a sentence.</p>
<p>Example:  Stream for Free, Pro, and Business plans</p>
<p>Without the Oxford comma, readers might misunderstand this header as free streaming for Pro and Business plans.</p>
