<p>We are making some improvements to our support experience. This could be causing a temporary issue linking your <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com">dashboard account</a> and your Cloudflare <a href="https://support.cloudflare.com/hc/requests">Help Center My Activities sign-on information</a>.</p>
<h2 id="accounts-with-paid-services">Accounts with paid services</h2>
<p>If you have an account with paid services:</p>
<ol>
<li>Open a case with us through our <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/support">support portal</a>.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>Technical support</strong> &gt; <strong>Account</strong> &gt; <strong>Support Platform</strong> &gt; <strong>Other</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Is your issue domain related?</strong>, select &quot;No&quot;.</li>
<li>For <strong>Summary</strong>, enter &quot;Can't access Cloudflare Help Center My Activities.&quot;</li>
<li>Select <strong>Add more details</strong> or <strong>Open a Case</strong>.</li>
<li>For <strong>Description</strong>, enter a brief description of the issue using at least 20 words.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Submit Case</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>This process will allow us to link your account inside our new support system.</p>
<h2 id="free-accounts">Free accounts</h2>
<p>If you are a free account customer, you do not have access to this feature, but you can get assistance in many other ways:</p>
<ul>
<li>By joining the conversation in our <a href="https://community.cloudflare.com/">Community forums</a>.</li>
<li>By joining our <a href="https://discord.cloudflare.com/">Discord server</a>.</li>
<li>By checking out our <a href="/">Documentation</a>.</li>
<li>By visiting the <a href="https://support.cloudflare.com/">Cloudflare Help Center</a>. There you will find our most frequently asked questions, steps to issue resolution, and more.</li>
</ul>
