<p>Cloudflare has observed that Internet Service Providers (ISPs) within Russia are systematically throttling traffic to websites and services, including those protected by Cloudflare.</p>
<p>This appears to restrict data transfer to approximately 16 KB per connection, which renders most websites, including those of our customers, inaccessible or unusable for visitors from Russia.</p>
<p>What to expect with your website:</p>
<ul>
<li>This is not due to a misconfiguration of your Cloudflare settings.</li>
<li>You will likely see a significant drop in traffic from users in Russia in your Cloudflare Analytics.</li>
<li>Your visitors in Russia may experience connection failures or sites that do not load properly.</li>
<li>As these actions are taken at the ISP level within Russia, we do not have the ability to restore Internet connectivity for Russia-based users.</li>
</ul>
<p>If you are a Cloudflare enterprise customer, contact your account team for further assistance.</p>
<p>For further details, refer to the <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/russian-internet-users-are-unable-to-access-the-open-internet/">Russian Internet users are unable to access the open Internet blog post</a>.</p>
