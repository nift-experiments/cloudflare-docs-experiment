<h2 id="errors-1006-1007-1008-or-1106-access-denied-your-ip-address-has-been-banned">Errors 1006, 1007, 1008 or 1106 Access Denied: Your IP address has been banned</h2>
<p>This error indicates that access is denied because your IP address has been banned.</p>
<h3 id="common-causes">Common causes</h3>
<p>A Cloudflare customer blocked traffic from your client or browser.</p>
<aside class="nb-aside note">
@markup("md", "content/.markup/bodies/14741.md")
</aside>
<h3 id="resolution">Resolution</h3>
<p>Request the website owner to investigate their Cloudflare security settings or allow your client IP address. Since the website owner blocked your request, Cloudflare support cannot override a customer's security settings.</p>
