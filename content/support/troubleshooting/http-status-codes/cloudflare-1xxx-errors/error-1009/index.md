<h2 id="errors-1009-access-denied-country-or-region-banned">Errors 1009 Access Denied: Country or region banned</h2>
<p>This error indicates that access to the website is denied from your country or region.</p>
<h3 id="common-causes">Common causes</h3>
<p>The owner of the website (for example, <code>example.com</code>) has banned the country or region your IP address from accessing the website.</p>
<h3 id="resolution">Resolution</h3>
<ul>
<li>If you are a site visitor, contact the site owner and ask them to allow your IP address.</li>
<li>If you are the site owner, ensure that the reported IP address is allowed under the <a href="/waf/tools/ip-access-rules/">IP Access rules</a> security feature.</li>
</ul>
