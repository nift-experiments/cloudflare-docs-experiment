<h2 id="error-1010-the-owner-of-this-website-has-banned-your-access-based-on-your-browser-s-signature">Error 1010: The owner of this website has banned your access based on your browser's signature</h2>
<p>This error indicates that access to the website is denied based on your browser's signature.</p>
<h3 id="common-cause">Common cause</h3>
<p>A website owner blocked your request based on your client's web browser.</p>
<h3 id="resolution">Resolution</h3>
<p>Notify the website owner of the blocking. If you cannot determine how to contact the website owner, lookup contact information for the domain via the <a href="https://lookup.icann.org/">Whois database</a>. Site owners can <a href="/waf/tools/browser-integrity-check/#disable-browser-integrity-check">turn off Browser Integrity Check</a> in the Security <strong>Settings</strong> page.</p>
