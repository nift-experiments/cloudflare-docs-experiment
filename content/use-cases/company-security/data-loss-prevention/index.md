<p>Sensitive data — Personally Identifiable Information (PII), payment card numbers, health records — can leave your network through employee uploads, SaaS misconfigurations, or compromised applications. Cloudflare One detects sensitive data patterns in traffic and blocks exfiltration in real time.</p>
<h2 id="solutions">Solutions</h2>
<h3 id="cloudflare-one">Cloudflare One</h3>
<p>Secure your organization with a cloud security platform that replaces legacy perimeters with Cloudflare's global network. <a href="/cloudflare-one/">Learn more about Cloudflare One</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Data detection</strong> - Identify sensitive data patterns — Personally Identifiable Information (PII), Payment Card Industry (PCI), Protected Health Information (PHI), and custom patterns — in traffic flowing through Gateway</li>
<li><strong>Inline protection</strong> - Block uploads or transfers of sensitive data in real time before they leave the network</li>
<li><strong>SaaS visibility</strong> - Discover which SaaS applications employees are using, including unsanctioned shadow IT</li>
<li><strong>Posture management</strong> - Identify misconfigurations and overly permissive sharing settings across connected SaaS apps</li>
</ul>
