<p>Building a video platform requires upload handling, encoding to multiple resolutions, adaptive bitrate delivery, and global distribution. Cloudflare Stream handles all of this — upload, transcode, store, and deliver — with no video infrastructure to manage.</p>
<h2 id="solutions">Solutions</h2>
<h3 id="stream">Stream</h3>
<p>Upload, encode, store, and deliver video with adaptive bitrate streaming. <a href="/stream/">Learn more about Stream</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Automatic encoding</strong> - Videos transcoded to multiple resolutions and formats on upload with no infrastructure to manage</li>
<li><strong>Adaptive bitrate</strong> - HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) and Dynamic Adaptive Streaming over HTTP (DASH) automatically adjusts quality to match viewer bandwidth</li>
<li><strong>Global delivery</strong> - Video served directly from Cloudflare's edge network without a separate Content Delivery Network (CDN)</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="stream-live">Stream Live</h3>
<p>Live streaming with automatic recording and instant playback. <a href="/stream/stream-live/">Learn more about Stream Live</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Live streaming</strong> - Low-latency live video with automatic recording and instant playback after the stream ends</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="realtimekit">RealtimeKit</h3>
<p>Add customizable live video and voice to web or mobile applications. <a href="/realtime/realtimekit/">Learn more about RealtimeKit</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Real-time communication</strong> - Add peer-to-peer video and audio directly to your application</li>
</ul>
