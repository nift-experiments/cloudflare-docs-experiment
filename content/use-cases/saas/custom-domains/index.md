<p>SaaS customers expect to use their own domains (for example, <code>app.theirdomain.com</code>) instead of a subdomain on your platform. Cloudflare SSL for SaaS provisions and renews SSL certificates automatically for every custom hostname, with no manual steps or customer action required.</p>
<h2 id="solutions">Solutions</h2>
<h3 id="cloudflare-for-platforms">Cloudflare for Platforms</h3>
<p>Extend Cloudflare's network and services to your customers. <a href="/cloudflare-for-platforms/">Learn more about Cloudflare for Platforms</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Custom domains</strong> - Customers bring their own domains and have them route to your platform with per-tenant configuration</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="ssl-for-saas">SSL for SaaS</h3>
<p>Provision and manage SSL certificates for custom customer domains. <a href="/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/">Learn more about SSL for SaaS</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Automatic SSL</strong> - Certificates provisioned and renewed automatically for every customer domain with no manual steps</li>
<li><strong>No customer action required</strong> - Cloudflare can complete domain validation without requiring action from the customer</li>
<li><strong>Scale</strong> - Support thousands of custom hostnames per domain without additional per-hostname infrastructure</li>
</ul>
