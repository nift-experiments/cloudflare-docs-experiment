They are connected to data centers outside of mainland China,</p><p>and these longer distances will lead to high latency and low availability,</p><p>creating a slow and poor user experience.</p><p>We can speed up these web services easily with Cloudflare China network solution,</p><p>with availability improving from 25% to 100% and response time speeding up from 300</p><p>milliseconds to just 50
