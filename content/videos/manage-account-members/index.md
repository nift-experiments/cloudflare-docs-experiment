<details class="nb-details"><summary>Transcript</summary><div class="nb-details-body video-transcript"><p>Account members are users who have access to your Cloudflare account.</p><p>Each member's access is controlled by policies which define their roles and the</p><p>scope of their permissions.</p><p>In order to grant access to account members.</p><p>You must be a super administrator with a verified email address.</p><p>In this video, we'll walk through how to view members,</p><p>add new members, edit their permissions, resend invitations,</p><p>and revoke access in Cloudflare dashboard.</p><p>Let's first take a look at how to view members.</p><p>Start by logging in to the Cloudflare dashboard and selecting your account.</p><p>Go to Manage Account Members.</p><p>Here you can see the list of current account members.</p><p>Now let's move on to adding account members.</p><p>On the members page to invite someone new, select invite.</p><p>Enter one or more email addresses.</p><p>Define the scope of their access.</p><p>Choose one or more roles such as administrator or billing.</p><p>Click continue to summary.</p><p>Review the details, then select invite.</p><p>If the person already has a Cloudflare account and you're on an enterprise plan,</p><p>you can use Direct Add instead of sending an email invitation.</p><p>If you want to change a member's access and permissions.</p><p>First open the record and select edit, adjust their roles or scope,</p><p>then select continue to summary.</p><p>Review the changes and select update.</p><p>If an invite is pending and the user hasn't accepted,</p><p>this is how you can resend it from the member's page.</p><p>Find a pending user, open the record and choose Resend Invite to revoke someone's</p><p>access. Expand their record.</p><p>Select, revoke and confirm.</p><p>If you were added as a member and you want to remove yourself.</p><p>Go to the member's page, locate your record and select leave.</p><p>If you're a super administrator and you need to leave the account,</p><p>you can invite another super administrator before you remove yourself.</p><p>You cannot delete the account itself, but other super administrators will retain</p><p>full privileges. Cloudflare also supports user groups,</p><p>which let you manage permissions for a set of members collectively.</p><p>Members inherit all roles assigned to the group in addition to any directly assigned</p><p>permissions. For more information on account members and user groups,</p><p>visit our developer documentation.</p></div></details>
