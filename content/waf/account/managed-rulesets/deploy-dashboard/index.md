<h2 id="configure-a-managed-ruleset">Configure a managed ruleset</h2>
<p>Configure a managed ruleset to define specific field values for one or more rules (for example, configure a rule with an action different from the action configured by Cloudflare). You can also turn off specific rules.</p>
<p>To skip one or more rules — or even entire WAF managed rulesets — for specific incoming requests, <a href="/waf/managed-rules/waf-exceptions/">add an exception</a>. Exceptions, also called skip rules, are shown as <em>Skip</em> rules in the <strong>Deployed managed rulesets</strong> list.</p>
