<h3 class="nb-aside-title" id="looking-for-zero-trust">Looking for Zero Trust?</h3>
@markup("md", "content/.markup/bodies/15775.md")
</aside>
<p>By default, 1.1.1.1:Faster Internet is configured to WARP mode. You can also configure it to only encrypt your DNS queries and leave the remaining traffic unencrypted.</p>
<h2 id="set-up-1-1-1-1-faster-internet">Set up 1.1.1.1: Faster Internet</h2>
<ol>
<li>Download <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cloudflare.onedotonedotonedotone">1.1.1.1: Faster Internet from Google Play</a> for free.</li>
<li>Launch 1.1.1.1: Faster Internet and accept the Terms of Service.</li>
<li>Toggle the <strong>WARP</strong> button to <strong>Connected</strong>.</li>
<li>Install the VPN profile that allows your phone to connect securely to 1.1.1.1.</li>
</ol>
<p>Your connection to the Internet and your DNS queries are now protected.</p>
<h2 id="encrypt-only-dns-queries">Encrypt only DNS queries</h2>
<p>After installing 1.1.1.1: Faster Internet, you may want to only encrypt your DNS queries and leave the remaining traffic unencrypted. If this is the case:</p>
<ol>
<li>Open 1.1.1.1: Faster Internet.</li>
<li>Toggle the WARP button and choose <strong>Switch to DNS only mode</strong>.</li>
<li>If the WARP toggle is disconnected, tap the <strong>menu</strong> button.</li>
<li>You will see two options: 1.1.1.1 and WARP. Select <strong>1.1.1.1</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>You are now using encryption only for your DNS queries.</p>
<h2 id="enable-1-1-1-1-for-families">Enable 1.1.1.1 for Families</h2>
<ol>
<li>Open 1.1.1.1: Faster Internet.</li>
<li>Tap the <strong>menu button</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Advanced</strong> &gt; <strong>Connection options</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>DNS settings</strong> &gt; <strong>1.1.1.1 for Families</strong>, select the option you want to use.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="how-to-remove-the-application">How to remove the application</h2>
<ol>
<li>Find the application on the home screen.</li>
<li>Touch and hold on the application tile.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Remove App</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Delete App</strong>.</li>
</ol>
