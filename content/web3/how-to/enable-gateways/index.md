<p>Before you can <a href="/web3/how-to/manage-gateways/#create-a-gateway">create a new gateway</a>, you need to subscribe to one or more gateways.</p>
<h2 id="create-new-subscription">Create new subscription</h2>
<p>To subscribe to a Web3 gateway (if you have not already subscribed):</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Web3</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Click <strong>Subscribe to Web3 Gateways</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose which gateways you want to subscribe to.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Proceed to Payment Details</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter your payment information (if not already attached to your account) and complete your purchase.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="manage-existing-subscription">Manage existing subscription</h2>
<p>To update an existing subscription or subscribe to an additional gateway:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Web3</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
<ol start="2">
<li>Click <strong>Manage Subscriptions</strong>.</li>
<li>To update existing gateway subscriptions, click <strong>Change</strong>. To purchase access to a new gateway, click <strong>Subscribe</strong>.</li>
</ol>
