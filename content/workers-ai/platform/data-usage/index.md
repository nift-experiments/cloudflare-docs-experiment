<p>Your inputs (e.g., text prompts, image submissions, audio files, etc.), outputs (e.g., generated text/images, translations, etc.), embeddings, and training data constitute Customer Content.</p>
<p>For Workers AI:</p>
<ul>
<li>You own, and are responsible for, all of your Customer Content.</li>
<li>Cloudflare does not make your Customer Content available to any other Cloudflare customer.</li>
<li>Cloudflare does not use your Customer Content to (1) train any AI models made available on Workers AI or (2) improve any Cloudflare or third-party services, and would not do so unless we received your explicit consent.</li>
<li>Your Customer Content for Workers AI may be stored by Cloudflare if you specifically use a storage service (e.g., R2, KV, DO, Vectorize, etc.) in conjunction with Workers AI.</li>
