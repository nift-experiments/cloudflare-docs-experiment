<p>Logs are an important component of a developer's toolkit to troubleshoot and diagnose application issues and maintaining system health. The Cloudflare Developer Platform offers many tools to help developers manage their application's logs.</p>
<h2 id="workers-logs-workers-observability-logs-workers-logs"><a href="/workers/observability/logs/workers-logs">Workers Logs</a></h2>
<p>Automatically ingest, filter, and analyze logs emitted from Cloudflare Workers in the Cloudflare dashboard.</p>
<h2 id="real-time-logs-workers-observability-logs-real-time-logs"><a href="/workers/observability/logs/real-time-logs">Real-time logs</a></h2>
<p>Access log events in near real-time. Real-time logs provide immediate feedback and visibility into the health of your Cloudflare Worker.</p>
<h2 id="tail-workers-workers-observability-logs-tail-workers"><a href="/workers/observability/logs/tail-workers">Tail Workers</a> <span class="nb-badge">Beta</span></h2>
<p>Tail Workers allow developers to apply custom filtering, sampling, and transformation logic to telemetry data.</p>
<h2 id="workers-logpush-workers-observability-logs-logpush"><a href="/workers/observability/logs/logpush">Workers Logpush</a></h2>
<p>Send Workers Trace Event Logs to a supported destination. Workers Logpush includes metadata about requests and responses, unstructured <code>console.log()</code> messages and any uncaught exceptions.</p>
