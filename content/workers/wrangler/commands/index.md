<p><a href="/workers/wrangler/">Wrangler</a> offers a number of commands to manage your Cloudflare Workers.</p>
<h2 id="workers-commands">Workers commands</h2>
<p>The core Wrangler commands for creating, developing, and deploying Workers are on the <a href="/workers/wrangler/commands/workers/">Workers commands page</a>. This includes <code>wrangler dev</code>, <code>wrangler deploy</code>, <code>wrangler versions</code>, and more.</p>
