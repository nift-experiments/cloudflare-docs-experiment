<p>Exporting your Zaraz configuration can be useful if you want to create a local backup or if you need to import it to another website. Zaraz provides an easy way to export and import your configuration.</p>
<h2 id="export-your-zaraz-configuration">Export your Zaraz configuration</h2>
<p>To export your Zaraz configuration:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Settings</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
2. Go to **Advanced**.
3. Click "Export" to download your configuration.
<h2 id="import-your-zaraz-configuration">Import your Zaraz configuration</h2>
<aside class="nb-aside caution">
@markup("md", "content/.markup/bodies/17610.md")
</aside>
<p>To import a Zaraz configuration:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Settings</strong> page.</li>
</ol>
<div class="nb-dash-button"></div>
2. Go to **Advanced**.
3. Click **Browse** to select your configuration file, and **Import** to import it.
