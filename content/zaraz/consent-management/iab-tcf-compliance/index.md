<p>The Zaraz Consent Management Platform is compliant with the IAB Transparency &amp; Consent Framework. Enabling this feature <a href="https://blog.google/products/adsense/new-consent-management-platform-requirements-for-serving-ads-in-the-eea-and-uk/">could be required</a> in order to serve Google Ads in the EEA and the UK.</p>
<p>The CMP ID of the approval is 433 and be can seen in the <a href="https://iabeurope.eu/cmp-list/">IAB Europe</a> website.</p>
<p>Using the Zaraz Consent Management Platform in IAB TCF Compliance Mode is opt-in.</p>

<li>In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the <strong>Consent</strong> page.</li>
