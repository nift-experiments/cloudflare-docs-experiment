<p>Zaraz can work in real-time. In this mode, every change you make is instantly published. You can also enable <a href="/zaraz/history/preview-mode/">Preview &amp; Publish mode</a>, which allows you to test your changes before you commit to them.</p>
<p>When enabling Preview &amp; Publish mode, you will also have access to <a href="/zaraz/history/versions/">Zaraz History</a>. Zaraz History shows you a list of all the changes made to your settings, and allows you to revert to any previous settings.</p>
