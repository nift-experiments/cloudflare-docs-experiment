## Consequences

1. Source count is not URL count: generated route families must also be captured.
2. MDX/JSX and Cloudflare components must be translated explicitly; unknown constructs fail loudly.
3. Original assets/fonts/icons are part of parity, not approximations.
4. Nimbus/Astro are implementation details; their rendered/behavioural output is the contract.
5. Remote/prebuild data must be frozen for reproducible final benchmarking.
6. Benchmark only equivalent sites; a faster incomplete site is not a win.
